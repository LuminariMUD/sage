Usage:  compose

@n

Compose is the command for bard's to interface with their

spells.  You can check how many spell slots you have total,

how many you have used and how many you have left over.

@n

Also compose is the command used to recover your slots when

you need, just type 'rest' then 'compose' to begin the spell

recovery.
