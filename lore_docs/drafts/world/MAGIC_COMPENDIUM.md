# The Magic Compendium: A Unified Theory of Power in Lumia

*As decreed by the Convergence Council, witnessed by representatives of the Arcane Universities, the Divine Temples, the Primal Circles, and the Mind's Eye Monastery*

*"Magic is not one truth told three ways, but three lies that become true through belief."*
—The Paradox of Power

## The Architecture of Impossibility

In the beginning, there was no magic. This is the first truth and the first lie.

Magic exists because the prison exists. When the Infinite Eternal split itself into Aeons and Khadhu, when creation and destruction collided, the impact shattered reality into three layers, each with its own physics, its own impossibilities made possible. What we call magic is simply the leakage between these layers—wounds that refuse to heal, cracks that let the impossible through.

## The Four Pillars of Power

### I. Arcane Magic: The Mathematics of Borrowing

**Source**: The Primal Realm, that space of pure possibility where concepts exist without form
**Practitioners**: Wizards, Sorcerers, Artificers
**Medium**: Crystallized Arcanite, spell formulae, focus objects
**Truth**: Every spell is a loan that must be repaid

Arcane magic functions through mathematical proof. When a wizard casts *fireball*, they are not creating fire but proving mathematically that fire already exists at the target location. The gestures and words are not mystical—they are solving equations in real-time, calculating the exact angle at which reality must be bent for possibility to leak through.

**The Arcanite Paradox**: Crystallized arcanite is literally frozen possibility, formed where the Primal Realm touched our world. The Crystal Dwarves of Nagburim depend on it utterly—at each dwarf's core lies a glowing Arcanite heart, a lattice of crystalline energy that sustains their being. This heart ties them irrevocably to Lumina itself; beyond the world's natural Arcanite field, their hearts slowly fade and they wither into lifeless crystal. Though wondrous, these hearts cannot be harvested to power magical artifacts, for their structure is too intricate and unlike any other form of Arcanite. Every piece mined weakens the barrier between realms. We are consuming our prison walls for power.

**Schools of Arcane Magic**:
- **Evocation**: Direct borrowing of elemental forces
- **Transmutation**: Rewriting the mathematical properties of matter
- **Illusion**: Creating false proofs that temporarily convince reality
- **Necromancy**: Borrowing from the debt of the already-dead
- **Conjuration**: Importing solutions from parallel calculations
- **Divination**: Reading ahead in reality's computation
- **Enchantment**: Inserting subroutines into consciousness
- **Abjuration**: Firewall protocols against other borrowings

### II. Divine Magic: The Politics of Possession

**Source**: Titan Pocket Planes, filtered through divine bureaucracy
**Practitioners**: Clerics, Paladins, Divine Souls
**Medium**: Prayer, faith, sacred symbols
**Truth**: Every miracle is a brief possession by something vast and hungry

Divine magic is not granted by gods but by Titans—vast entities that exist in pocket dimensions, pressing against our reality like hands against glass. The gods are middle managers, translating Titan-force into comprehensible miracles. When a cleric heals, a Titan briefly inhabits their body, reshaping reality from within.

**The Worship Engine**: Prayer doesn't make Titans stronger—it makes them more present. Each prayer is a thread. Enough threads, and the Titan can step through entirely. This has happened three times. Each time, the summoning civilization ceased to exist.

**Divine Domains** (and their Titan sources):
- **Life/Death**: Drawn from the Titan of Endings and Beginnings
- **War/Peace**: The Titan of Conflict Resolution
- **Knowledge/Trickery**: The Titan of Hidden Truths
- **Nature/Tempest**: The Elemental Titans (multiple entities)
- **Light/Darkness**: The Titan of Perception

### III. Primal Magic: The Memory of What Never Was

**Source**: Lumia itself—the world-dream of a sleeping entity
**Practitioners**: Druids, Rangers, Shamans, Bards
**Medium**: Sacred sites, bloodlines, ancient songs
**Truth**: Reality can be convinced to remember being different

Primal magic doesn't cast spells—it whispers suggestions to the dreaming world. A druid's grove isn't cultivated; it's a place where the world remembers being primordial forest. When successful, primal magic doesn't change the present—it retroactively changes the past so the present was always different.

**The Three Memories**:
- **Stone Memory** (Druidic): The earth remembers every age
- **Blood Memory** (Shamanic): Creatures remember all their ancestors
- **Song Memory** (Bardic): Sound remembers the words that sang reality into being

### IV. Psionic Magic: The Refusal to Participate

**Source**: The practitioner's rejection of consensus reality
**Practitioners**: Psions, Mystics, Aberrant Minds
**Medium**: Pure will, mental discipline, crystallized doubt
**Truth**: Reality is optional for those who truly believe it

Psionics is not magic—it is the absence of agreement with reality's rules. A psion doesn't cast *telekinesis*; they simply refuse to accept that objects require physical contact to move. This is why psionics works differently from all other magic—it's not borrowing, channeling, or remembering. It's conscientiously objecting to physics.

**Psionic Disciplines**:
- **Psychokinetics**: Refusing to accept distance and mass
- **Telepathy**: Denying the privacy of thought
- **Metacreativity**: Insisting things exist without cause
- **Clairsentience**: Rejecting the limitations of perception
- **Psychometabolism**: Disagreeing with biology
- **Psychoportation**: Vetoing the continuity of space

## The Synthesis: Where All Paths Converge

### The Pattern Beneath

Reality itself is mathematical—a vast computation where every location can be reduced to coordinates, every coordinate yields consistent terrain when processed through the same formulae. Magic is hacking this code:

- **Arcane**: Exploiting bugs in the physics engine
- **Divine**: Using admin privileges granted by Titans
- **Primal**: Editing the save file of history
- **Psionic**: Running unauthorized modifications

### The Loom Integration

All magic ultimately affects the Loom of Aether, but each path pulls different threads:

- **Arcane** pulls from **Possibility** (future threads)
- **Divine** pulls from **Will** (present threads)
- **Primal** pulls from **Memory** (past threads)
- **Psionics** cuts threads entirely, creating gaps in the weave

### The Shadowmoon Amplification

During Shadowmoon rises, all magic malfunctions because the moon's probability-based orbit interferes with reality's calculations:

- **Arcane** spells borrow from wrong times or places
- **Divine** magic connects to Titans that shouldn't exist
- **Primal** magic remembers futures that contradict the present
- **Psionic** powers begin affecting the practitioner's own existence

## The Intersection Phenomena

### Dual-Path Practitioners

Those who master two paths experience unique phenomena:

**Arcane-Divine** (Theurges):
- Can borrow directly from Titan realms
- Risk becoming self-made gods
- Often simply discorporate into pure concept

**Divine-Primal** (Hierophants):
- Access pre-god compacts of power
- Can bypass divine bureaucracy
- Hunted by jealous deities

**Arcane-Primal** (Geomancers):
- Cast spells backward through time
- Create paradox magic that shouldn't exist
- Risk editing themselves out of reality

**Psionic-Any** (Mind Witches):
- Reality actively attacks them
- Powers function but cause cascading errors
- Become walking paradoxes

### Triple-Path Masters

Only seven beings have ever mastered three paths. Six no longer exist in any recognizable form. The seventh is the Prisoner, and their mastery is why they had to be imprisoned—they could remake reality by borrowing possibility, channeling Titan-force, and remembering the universe differently, all while refusing to accept any limitations.

## The Forbidden Knowledge

### The Null Path

There exists an anti-magic that denies magic's possibility. Practitioners called "Deniers" don't cast spells—they state facts that make magic impossible. The problem: they eventually convince themselves out of existence.

### The Fifth Path (Theoretical)

Some scholars theorize a fifth path that would unite all others—the ability to directly manipulate the Loom itself. This is what the Prisoner discovered, and why they had to be imprisoned. Anyone who achieves this unity would have the power to:

- Rewrite the laws of physics (Arcane mastery)
- Become their own Titan (Divine mastery)
- Reshape history itself (Primal mastery)
- Reject all limitations (Psionic mastery)
- Deny their own containment (Null mastery)
