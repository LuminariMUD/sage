Summoner Abilities:

Eidolon: Can summon a powerful creature that takes the form desired by the summoner. Has same alignment as summoner.  Can be improved with evolutions. Must be assigned a base form when summoning.

Life Link: When the eidolon takes enough damage to kill it, the extra damage is instead taken from the summoner, unless that amount would reduce the summoner’s hp below zero. This can only happen once every 10 minutes. Needs a PRF flag.

Summon Monster: The summoner can cast summon monster at will, with the power of the summon increasing with level.

Bond Senses: The summoner can take control of their eidolon (similar to switch command).

Shield Ally: When in the same room, and the eidolon is not grappled, helpless, paralyzed, stunned or unconscious, the summoner gains a +2 shield bonus to AC, and a +2 circumstance bonus to saves.

Maker’s call: The summoner can call his summons to him for half the normal move cost.

Transposition: The summoner can swap places with his eidolon.

Aspect: The summoner can use two evolution points to add an evolution benefit to himself.

Greater Shield Ally: As Shield Ally, except the above bonuses apply to all allies in the same room, and the summoner gains +4 bonuses instead of +2.

Life Bond: As long as the eidolon is in the same room and alive, any damage the summoner takes is redirected to the eidolon instead. Needs a PRF flag.

Merge Forms: The summoner’s eidolon is dismissed and the summoner gains all of the eidolon’s evolutions, as well as their STR, DEX and CON ability scores. This lasts 1 round per summoner level, and is usable once per day. Once the duration expires, the eidolon can be resummoned.

Greater Aspect: As aspect, but can take up to 6 evolution points.

Grand Eidolon: 4 additional evolution points.

Eidolon Evolutions:

Unless otherwise stated evolutions cannot stack.

Extra attacks will only add strength damage and damroll. They have a crit threat range of 20 and a crit multiplier of x2.

When an extra attack requires a number of limbs, those limbs can only be used for that attack.  For example a creature with claw attacks and pincer attacks would need 4 arm limbs. To have a tail slap and a sting attack, they’d need 2 tails.

An Eidolon can have a maximum number of evolution attacks based on this table: Eidolons – d20PFSRD

1-Point Evolutions

Basic magic: Will randomly cast the following spells (with no cast time): acid splash, dancing lights, daze, flare, light, ray of frost, touch of fatigue.

Bite: Gains an extra bite attack at 1d6 points of damage. Piercing damage.

Bleed: Attacks cause 1 point of bleed damage per round for 6 rounds. The bleed damage will stack up to a maximum of 6 bleed damage per round.

Claws: Gain two claw attacks at 1d4 damage (1d6 if large, 1d8 if huge). Slashing damage. Requires 2 limbs.

Climb: +30 to climb skills.

Gills: Can breathe under water.

Hooves: 2 hoof attacks at 1d4 damage (1d6 if large, 1d8 if huge). Bludgeoning damage. Requires 2 limbs.

Improved Damage: Increase the damage dice by one size for the selected attack type. Canbe chosen multiple times, but each rank is specific to a related eidolon attack.

Improved Natural Armor: Gain +2 natural AC. Can take every 5 summoner levels and will stack.

Magic Attacks: All attacks are considered made by a magic weapon and gain +1 to hit and damroll.

Mount: The eidolon can be mounted if large enough.

Pincers: Gain 2 pincer attacks at 1d6 damage (1d8 if large, 2d6 if huge). Bludgeoning damage. +2 to grapple checks. Requires 2 limbs.

Pounce: can gain full attack after a charge.

Reach: Gain a single free attack when starting combat, before initiative is rolled.

Resistance to Acid: Gain dr 5/- against acid damage.  Increases by 5 per 5 summoner levels, max of 15/-.

Resistance to Cold: Gain dr 5/- against cold damage.  Increases by 5 per 5 summoner levels, max of 15/-.

Resistance to Electricity: Gain dr 5/- against electricity damage.  Increases by 5 per 5 summoner levels, max of 15/-.

Resistance to Fire: Gain dr 5/- against fire damage.  Increases by 5 per 5 summoner levels, max of 15/-.

Resistance to Sonic: Gain dr 5/- against sonic damage.  Increases by 5 per 5 summoner levels, max of 15/-.

Scent: Can track others if target within a certain distance.

Skilled: Gains a +2 to all skills.  Can be selected every 5 summoner levels and stacks.

Slam: Can perform a single slam attack at 1d8 damage (2d6 if large, 2d8 if huge).  Bludgeoning damage.

Slippery: +4 to CMD to resist grapple

Sticky: +4 CMB to grapple other

Sting: Gain a 1d4 (1d6 if large, 1d8 if huge) sting attack. Requires tail. Piercing damage.

Swim: +30 to swim skill.

Tail: Has a tail. +2 to acrobatics skill.

Tail slap: Can perform tail slap attack. 1d6 damage (1d8 if large, 2d6 if huge).  Requires tail. Bludgeoning damage

Tentacle: Can perform a tentacle attack at 1d4 damage (1d6 large 1d8 huge). Bludgeoning damage

Wing Buffet: Gain two wing buffet attacks at 1d4 (1d6 large 1d8 huge) damage each. Bludgeoning damage. Requires 2 wings.

2-Point Evolutions

Attribute Increase: Gain +2 to strength, dex or con.  Can be taken once every 6 summoner levels per stat.

Smite: 10% chance per attack to deal extra 3d6 damage.  Holy damage type for good/neutral summoners, unholy for evil.

Channel Resistance: Undead only. +2 to saves vs. channel energy. +4 at summoner level 7.

Energy Attacks: +1d6 element damage, chosen when taking the evo.  Choose between fire, cold, acid, electricity and sonic damage types.
