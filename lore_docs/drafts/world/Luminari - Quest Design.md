Luminari - Quest Design Document

Purpose: 

This document describes the quests in Luminari, their limitations and interactions and any other information that is relevant to the questing portion of the game.  The goal is to make sure that all quests follow the theme and style of the game and that all interactions between different quests throughout the progression are documented.  This is mainly to allow the development of additional quest lines and side quests that interact with the currently existing system.

Definitions:

  Campaign - A group of quests that are linked together into a quest line - Campaigns set the story and theme for the game and tie all of the zones together.

  Quest -  A group of tasks that are grouped together, providing direction for players to experience game content.  Usually, quests are defined using the autoquest system.

  

Outstanding Issues: 

Integration - HLQuests and Autoquests MUST be integrated and a simple, standard interface developed.  It is far too confusing at the moment for new players to handle different quest systems as well as all of the other systems in the game.
