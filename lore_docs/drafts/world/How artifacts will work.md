# How artifacts will work

*Converted from: How artifacts will work.docx*

Artifacts will have a powerful and unique role in Lumia. There will be a revolving list and amount of artifacts existing within Lumia during a period of time. The time period will be set by  senior staff, and will not be definite (meaning it may change from one week to one month to many weeks, depending on current events and workload).

There will be three ways to obtain artifacts:

(Common) Solving a riddle or killing a guardian of an artifact. These will be elaborate and change each update, which I will do myself if I have to. The riddle will pertain to lore within Lumia, and normally there will be a special command when you get to the built room or area the artifact could be located. Often there should be a guardian appropriate to the level of the actual artifact. Artifacts SHOULD NOT be able to be traded or given away, to ensure a lower level artifact will not just be harvested by a higher level player and transferred to a lower level counterpart of their own. Of course there will be ways for players to try and get around this, but the practice of defeating a guardian for someone else should be highly dissuaded.

(Uncommon) A titan could bestow an artifact to a mortal, but this should only be done after undergoing an exhaustive quest, the trading of something very valuable, or another equally taxing thing. This should not happen often, as players will believe there is favoritism if they feel they deserved an artifact over another player (a common example of this is, “But I roleplay my cleric of <insert Titan’s name> better, and I stay true to my alignment better, and <insert other reason> better.”) It’s a common thing for players to be jealous over others obtaining something they cannot, and therefore the staff should really be wary of not appearing to favor one over another.

(Rare) Player killing should be able to lead to the obtaining of an artifact, but this should be HIGHLY regulated. Artifacts are something that others would want, but it is NOT a valid reason to start combat with another player. This should also be warranted that railroading a player into RP that will lead to player combat is also not a valid way. The best solution for this would be that if the attacking player intends to take the artifact from another player, they should alert a staff member and give them the reasons for the PK, and get a final decision from them. The defending player does not need to be aware of the interaction between the staff member and the attacking player, but all other PK rules apply. Since most players who would PK not see eye to eye on ideals or moral stances, it is even rarer that the artifact would be desirable to the other player. Either way, as with all PK, if not done correctly, actions should be taken by staff to fix the problem.

As for artifacts themselves, there will be a certain amount of hindrances to get them. All should have a level range, meaning a player too low cannot use it, and a player too high may find the cost of keeping it too much. Artifacts should be unique in the world, meaning another cannot be created for any reason during it’s time on Lumia. If it’s lost, it’s lost. I would also suggest there be a decay time for how long it can remain on the ground, again to dissuade transferring it. All artifacts will have what is known as “artifact possession”, meaning every day the player must do something or there are negative consequences, ranging from the item disappearing, or it becoming cursed and incredibly useless until the player satisfies it, or the item just flat out killing the player if it’s needs are not met. This will be balanced by an universal timer known by everyone for when artifacts must have their needs met, so no one can claim ignorance. For players not online, there will be an xp and/or gold loss for every time the needs are not met and the player is offline, to dissuade logging out to not have to deal with the consequences. This should be on par with the xp loss of a death, something that hurts the player but doesn’t obliterate them. If the player feels they won’t be able to log in for a while, they should forfeit the artifact, and the staff should make reasonable accommodations for players who could not log in for periods due to an emergency (example, John the Warrior’s player gets hospitalized while having an artifact. That period was especially long, say 2 months, and when he now logs in, he’s at half the levels he normally had). A simple screenshot and email, or a staff member online should be able to fix this easily, and while not every player will love the idea, it’s a compromise to stop abuse.

In addition to “artifact possession”, there is also an artifact’s natural alignment. If a player holds onto an artifact for more than 1 real week, their alignment should change to that of the artifacts, and should be roleplayed correctly. While many would complain that classes with alignment requirements may be hindered by this, well, that’s the point. Paladins can always atone and bards can make a journey to get back in touch with their roots. The whole idea behind artifacts are that they are more powerful than mortals themselves, and this is one of the most commonly used and valid ways to show that it’s influence is stronger than mortal willpower, and that being greedy with an artifact is never a good idea.

Finally, a player who takes possession of two artifacts explodes. Literally. The energies build up inside him, everyone in the room they are in dies, and the two artifacts are lost during that cycle. Being greedy with an artifact is never a good idea. I’d prefer this one be hidden until someone actually does it, but up  to staff’s discretion. 

There should never be more than 5% of players in possession of artifacts, and never more than 10% of the total active playerbase worth’s of artifacts in the world.who

Side note: Eventually there may be players who have been important to the story, or lore. If/when they retire their characters. we should ask them what their favorite or most useful item was, and make an artifact of it. This way the artifact list keeps expanding and players will happily see their characters live on in the server. It also encourages character rotation!
