# The Five Locks: Guardians of Oblivion

*Compiled from the Testament of Serane, Last of the Luminari, with annotations by the Circle of Binding*

## The Nature of the Prison

The prison that holds the Prisoner is not, as many believe, a cage of crystalline arcanite buried deep beneath Lumia's surface. The prison *is* Lumia—every grain of sand, every drop of water, every breath of wind participates in the binding. The Five Locks are not doors to be opened but fundamental forces that must remain in balance, lest the equation that holds the Prisoner collapse into singularity.

Each Lock manifests in three ways: as a physical location, as a metaphysical principle, and as a living guardian who embodies its essence. To break a Lock, one must corrupt all three aspects simultaneously—a feat that should be impossible, yet the Prisoner's influence seeps through the calculations like water through stone.

## The First Lock: The Elevation Throne

### Physical Manifestation
*Mount Aetherspine, the Sword That Pierces Heaven*

Rising thirteen thousand feet above the Cerulean Wastes, Mount Aetherspine is not merely the tallest peak in Lumia—it is ambition made stone. The mountain grows three inches each year, as if still striving to escape the earth's embrace. At its summit lies the Elevation Throne, a seat carved from a single crystal of frozen lightning.

Those who attempt the climb report that the mountain resists them personally. Gravity increases with each step. The air thins beyond what altitude should dictate. Climbers speak of hearing their own doubts whispered back to them in voices they've never heard but somehow recognize.

### Metaphysical Principle
*The Binding of Aspiration*

The Elevation Lock constrains vertical movement in all its forms—not just physical climbing but the ascension of souls, the hierarchy of power, the very concept of "above" and "below." It ensures that nothing can rise high enough to see the true shape of the prison from outside.

### The Guardian: Skylord Icarus the Twice-Fallen

Once mortal, Icarus achieved the impossible twice—he climbed Aetherspine and claimed the Throne, then fell, then climbed again. Now he exists in perpetual descent, forever falling from the summit yet never reaching the ground. Those who glimpse him see a figure wreathed in broken wings, tumbling through clouds that part and reform in his wake.

He speaks only in paradoxes: "To rise is to fall. To fall is to rise. The summit is the base. The base is the summit."

## The Second Lock: The Depth Crown

### Physical Manifestation
*The Abyssal Reach, Where Water Forgets Its Name*

In the Darkling Sea, forty miles from any shore, lies a whirlpool that has spun since before recorded history. The Abyssal Reach descends beyond measurement—expeditions have dropped weighted lines for miles without finding bottom. The water here behaves wrong: it flows upward along the edges while plummeting at the center, creating a spiral that extends both up and down simultaneously.

At the mathematical center of the vortex, never moving despite the churning waters, floats the Depth Crown—a circlet of crystallized pressure that exists at all depths simultaneously.

### Metaphysical Principle
*The Binding of Foundation*

Where the Elevation Lock controls ascension, the Depth Lock governs descent, foundations, and the concept of "beneath." It prevents anything from sinking so deep that it passes through the bottom of reality and emerges somewhere else. It maintains the boundary between "here" and "nowhere."

### The Guardian: The Leviathan of Forgotten Names

No one has seen the Leviathan entire. Sailors report glimpsing scales the size of houses, eyes that hold the darkness between stars, tentacles that move through water without disturbing it. The creature exists partially in the Abyssal Reach and partially somewhere else—somewhere that might be the space between spaces.

The Leviathan collects the names of drowned things. Every ship, every sailor, every creature that sinks into the Reach has its name swallowed and added to the Leviathan's hoard. Some say if you could speak all these names at once, the Leviathan would be forced to surface completely, bringing with it whatever lies beneath the bottom of the world.

## The Third Lock: The Temporal Anchor

### Physical Manifestation
*The Chronos Garden, Where All Seasons Meet*

Hidden in the Temporal Peaks (mountains that exist in several time periods simultaneously), the Chronos Garden defies conventional understanding. Here, spring flowers bloom beside autumn leaves while winter frost and summer heat occupy the same space. Visitors age and grow younger with each step, experiencing their entire timeline in fragments.

At the garden's heart grows the Anchor Tree, whose roots extend into the past and whose branches reach into the future. Its trunk exists only in the present moment, making it invisible to most observers who cannot perfectly occupy "now."

### Metaphysical Principle
*The Binding of Sequence*

The Temporal Lock maintains causality, ensuring that cause precedes effect, that birth comes before death, that questions precede answers. Without it, the Prisoner could simply reshape history so that they were never imprisoned, or fast-forward to a time when the prison has decayed.

### The Guardian: The Oracle of Infinite Presents

The Oracle exists in all moments of her life simultaneously. When she speaks, her words emerge from child, maiden, mother, and crone voices layered together. She remembers everything that will happen and predicts everything that has occurred.

Her prophecies are always accurate but never useful, as she provides answers to questions that haven't been asked yet, or responds to queries from parallel timelines. "You will not ask the question," she might say, "that would have saved everyone, because you asked this one instead."

## The Fourth Lock: The Dream Gate

### Physical Manifestation
*The Somnium Sanctum, The Temple of Sleeping Gods*

Located on an island that only exists when no one is looking for it, the Somnium Sanctum houses the Dream Gate—a doorway that leads not to another place but to another state of being. The temple itself shifts between architectural styles, as if dreaming of all the temples it could have been.

Within sleep those who have achieved perfect lucidity—aware they dream but choosing not to wake. They maintain the boundary between dream and reality through their conscious unconsciousness.

### Metaphysical Principle
*The Binding of Consciousness*

The Dream Lock separates the real from the imagined, the conscious from the unconscious, the observer from the observed. It prevents the Prisoner from simply dreaming themselves free or convincing the universe that their imprisonment is merely a nightmare from which all must wake.

### The Guardian: The Congregation of Lucid Sleepers

Seven monks who have slept for seven centuries, their bodies preserved by dreams of immortality. They share a collective dream in which they constantly rebuild the walls of reality, patching holes where nightmare bleeds into the waking world.

If even one were to wake, their section of the dream-wall would collapse, allowing unreality to flood in. If all seven woke at once, the distinction between dream and waking would cease entirely. They know this, which is why they dream of never waking, even as their bodies slowly transform into living stone.

## The Fifth Lock: The Memory Chain

### Physical Manifestation
*The Archive Eternal, Where History Refuses to Die*

Built into a mountain that exists partially in the past, the Archive Eternal contains every memory ever formed in Lumia. The building extends infinitely inward, larger inside than the mountain that contains it. Its shelves hold not books but crystallized moments—memories given physical form.

The Memory Chain itself runs through the Archive's heart: an endless series of links, each one forged from a forgotten tragedy. The chain has no beginning and no end, wrapping through dimensions that don't properly exist.

### Metaphysical Principle
*The Binding of Identity*

The Memory Lock maintains the continuity of self, ensuring that beings remain who they are rather than becoming who they were or who they might be. It prevents the Prisoner from simply forgetting they are imprisoned, or remembering a version of themselves that was never bound.

### The Guardian: The Librarian of Lost Things

No one remembers the Librarian's original name, including the Librarian themselves. They exist as a living index of everything forgotten—every lost love, every abandoned hope, every path not taken. Their body is composed of shadow and whispered words, constantly shifting as memories flow through them.

The Librarian cannot create new memories, living in an eternal present informed only by the memories of others. They guard the Memory Chain by ensuring that some things remain forgotten, for if certain memories were recovered—memories of the time before the prison, of the Prisoner's true name, of the word that could undo all bindings—the Chain would shatter like spun sugar.

## The Interactions

The Five Locks do not operate independently. They form a metaphysical web, each supporting and reinforcing the others:

- **Elevation** and **Depth** define the vertical axis of reality
- **Temporal** and **Dream** control the nature of experience
- **Memory** binds them all together through continuity

When the Shadowmoon rises, its gravitational influence causes minute distortions in the Lock alignments. The Elevation Throne shifts imperceptibly downward. The Abyssal Reach spins fractionally slower. The Temporal Anchor skips a microsecond. The Dream Gate blurs at its edges. The Memory Chain forgets a single link, only to remember it later.

These distortions are growing stronger.

## The Prophecy of Unmaking

*As recorded in the Testament of Serane:*

"When mountains bow and oceans rise,  
When present, past, and future die,  
When dreamers wake and wakers sleep,  
When memory forgets to keep,  
Then shall the Five become the None,  
And Many-Who-Are-One be One."

The Crystal Dwarves claim to have calculated the exact date when the distortions will exceed the Locks' tolerance. They refuse to share this knowledge, saying only that "some dooms are made certain by their naming."

## The Sixth Lock (Theoretical)

Whispers speak of a Sixth Lock, one that even the Luminari didn't know they created. Some say it is Free Will itself—that the prison exists only because we believe it should. Others claim it is the collective unconscious of all sentient beings, our shared agreement that the Prisoner should remain bound.

If this Sixth Lock exists, its guardian is everyone and no one. Its location is everywhere and nowhere. Its principle is the simple, desperate hope that tomorrow will come.

But hope, as the poets know, is the most fragile lock of all.

*Addendum by an unknown hand:*

The Locks are weakening. Not from without, but from within. Each guardian grows weary. Each principle develops exceptions. Each location shifts from where it should be.

The Prisoner does not struggle against their bonds. They wait, patient as mathematics, knowing that all equations tend toward equilibrium.

And equilibrium, in this case, means release.
