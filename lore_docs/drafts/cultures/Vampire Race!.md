# Vampire Race!

*Converted from: Vampire Race!.docx*

I was thinking that it could just start as like a complaint of a peasant or something. The player goes looking for the vampire. Maybe they have to kill it. After being killed, the vampire reforms and then offers the player the chance to become a vampire themselves, which would then start the quest... conditions that must be met. People/things the vampire wants killed, objects of power he wants for himself. Maybe even people he wants protected, or items of sentimental value he wants recovered, from his life before he was a vampire. aybe a great great granddaughter he has somewhere, and he wants to protect her, or bring her some kind of riches or special item.

1. The player comes across a farm where the family and livestock have all been killed and bled dry. All except for one of the children, an older youth around 15 or so, who explains that the family's farm was attacked by a group of vampires, and he only survived because he was knocked out and fell under some debris. The player can then search the farm for clues as to what happened and where the attackers were based out of. I still have to figure out this 'puzzle' part of what the clues are and where it leads them.

2. The player goes to the vampire's lair and has to defeat a certain number of vampires there, and then the vampire's leader. Once the leader is dead, the players come to understand that the true leader was being held against his will after a coup was performed. The new leader was much more vicious and violent, hence the killings on the farm instead of only feeding when necessary and to refrain from killing where possible. The vampire thanks the player, and offers to make them a vampire if they will prove themselves worthy, beyond the simple violence of killing a vampire lair. So he sends the player off to perform a few tasks. They might not all be killing something, there may be a puzzle to solve, a person to help and a villain to kill.

3.After they've gained the vampire's trust and respect, he now reveals to them his story of becoming a vampire and his lamentations, and that for the player to fully understand what they'll be giving up, he wants them to investigate his past. Visit his old home, and obtain a precious heirloom therein. Talk to a great granddaughter of his about his now-family-legacy. Visit a beautiful landscape high on a mountain, overlooking a waterfall in brilliant sunlight, something they'll never see again.

4. Before they become a vampire, he wants them to know of a precious vampire secret, of which only the most powerful of vampire's gain knowledge of, and that is a specially crafted cloak that will protect the vampire from sunlight. In order to create one, they will have to collect a number of item components and bring them to the vampire. This will tie into the vampire cloak idea we discussed on Discord. 5. Once all has been done, he gives them one final chance to change their mind, and if they refuse to change their mind, he makes them a vampire.
