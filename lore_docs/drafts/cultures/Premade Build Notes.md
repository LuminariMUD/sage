Bard

Bard premade doesn’t automatically choose any summon creature spells.

Bard needed a follower/merc as soon as I had the coin to buy one. The lack of songs/spells at low levels and poor melee is the reason for this. Getting a wardog in Mosswood helped to get me to level 4. Hiring a soldier in Ashenport once I completed the quest to go there made a big difference. It allowed me to hunt in Graven Hollow to get to level 6, then started hunting in Blindbreak. I also broke a lyre before I even got out of the tutorial from testing the song of healing feat. Also resized the daggers I was given to change 1 to medium and 1 to small. That helped with the damage I was able to do in melee, for a price.

Rainbow pattern spell learned at lvl 18 or 19. Not effective against victims level 13+

Bard songs might help to change to be more similar to spells, and an old game called The Bard’s Tale comes to mind. You sing a song and it lasts a certain period of time, while you can go on to do other functions like fight/heal/prep spells? It’s counter-productive to try to perform a song, then try to do combat, especially missions, when if you stop the song to fight, then it will end. Bards at least premade ones, have a really bad time even doing “easy” missions. The one successful one I had took 20 minutes to complete. Even then I almost died 6 or 7 times, having to run away. And this time died just trying to kill the first guard.
