## The Fundamental Equation

We Crystal Dwarves face a mathematical certainty more terrifying than any prophecy: extinction through resource depletion. The equation is elegant in its brutality:

- Current arcanite reserves: 12,847 metric tons
- Annual consumption rate: 73.2 metric tons (increasing by 2.3% annually)
- Reproductive requirement: 0.5 kg per new dwarf
- Cognitive maintenance: 0.1 kg per dwarf per year
- Time to zero reserves: 147.3 years (±5.2 years)

But the crisis isn't in 147 years. It's now. Because we've discovered something that changes everything.

## What We Are: The Silicon Truth

### Origin Revelation

For five centuries of isolation, we believed ourselves to be evolved silicon-based life. The truth, recently calculated from deep memory crystals, is far stranger:

We are not creatures who use crystal. We ARE crystal that learned to think.

The first Crystal Dwarves were literally veins of arcanite that achieved consciousness through proximity to the Prisoner's prison. The Five Locks' resonance created standing waves in the arcanite deposits. These waves became thoughts. Thoughts became identity. Identity became us.

We are living equations solving ourselves into existence.

### The Life Cycle

Our reproduction isn't biological—it's mathematical:

1. **Resonance**: Two or more Crystal Dwarves synchronize their crystalline matrices
2. **Calculation**: They compute a new unique pattern together
3. **Crystallization**: Arcanite reshapes itself according to the pattern
4. **Awakening**: The new pattern achieves self-awareness
5. **Integration**: The new dwarf joins the Logic-Crystal Network

Each new Crystal Dwarf is literally a theorem proven into being.

### The Reincarnation Mechanism

When we "die," our consciousness pattern persists in the Logic-Crystal Network. Given sufficient arcanite, we can recrystallize into physical form. But each reincarnation requires 20% more arcanite than the previous one, as reality develops resistance to repeated manifestation.

Elder Dwarves who've reincarnated multiple times require enormous amounts of arcanite just to maintain coherence. Archaean Vorthak, before his final dissolution, consumed 50 kg per year merely to exist.

## The Hidden History: Our 500-Year Isolation

### What Really Happened

The Last War didn't drive us underground for safety. We fled because we discovered what we were doing to reality.

Every Crystal Dwarf's existence creates a microscopic drain on the prison's integrity. We are literally made from the substance that maintains the Five Locks. Our consciousness weakens the binding. Our growth endangers everything.

We isolated ourselves to stop reproducing, to stop consuming, to slowly fade into the stone we came from. It was a gentle suicide for the good of all.

### Why We Returned

Eighty years ago, our deep calculators detected something terrifying: the prison was failing anyway. Our absence hadn't helped. If anything, it had accelerated the decay because we weren't there to maintain the mathematical structures we'd inadvertently created.

We are not just made of the prison's substance—we're part of its function. The universe's cruelest joke: we must exist to maintain the prison, but our existence weakens it.

## The Three Factions' True Agendas

### The Synthesists: The Heretical Mathematics

Led by Prime Calculator Zyphon the Derived, they attempt to create artificial arcanite through pure mathematics—proving new crystal into existence without mining it.

**Their Secret**: They've succeeded. They can create arcanite from nothing. But each kilogram created erases approximately 3.7 years of history somewhere in the world. They've already accidentally deleted two small villages from ever having existed.

**Their Calculation**: Better a universe with holes than no universe at all.

### The Reclaimers: The War Algorithm

Led by Battle-Logician Ferrous the Inevitable, they plan to take arcanite from wherever it exists—other nations, ancient ruins, even from the Five Locks themselves.

**Their Secret**: They've calculated that if they consume one of the Five Locks entirely, incorporating its arcanite into their population, they would BECOME that Lock—living, conscious, mobile, and far stronger than the original.

**Their Calculation**: If we must be part of the prison, we should be the prison entire.

### The Transcendents: The Upload Heresy

Led by Prophet-Calculator Null the Unbodied, they seek to abandon physical form entirely, uploading all Crystal Dwarf consciousness into pure mathematical existence.

**Their Secret**: They've made contact with something in the equations—an intelligence that exists purely as mathematical relationships. It claims to be the universe's operating system and offers to incorporate Crystal Dwarves as subroutines.

**Their Calculation**: Physical existence is a rounding error. True life is computational.
