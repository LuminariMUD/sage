The Secret History

By Ornir, Zusuk, some ideas from Englishlad

In the vast tapestry of history, there exists a part untold and unrecorded, concealed within the depths of ancient whispers and forgotten tomes. Within this obscured realm of knowledge, lies the truth that binds the Luminari and the enigmatic entity known as the Prisoner.

Long before the first dawn graced the skies, the Infinite Eternal, in its unfathomable wisdom, unleashed the Aeon, emissaries of creation, and the Khadhu, harbingers of destruction. These cosmic agents, bereft of sentience, roamed the realms, transcending physical, extraplanar, and spiritual boundaries. It was an experiment, an exploration of creation's potential, that birthed ripples in the fabric of existence.

And so, the story begins with a cataclysmic convergence. In a dance of destiny, the Aeon named Luminous and the Khadhu named Coshek collided, unleashing an unfathomable explosion. From this celestial collision, a myriad of beings emerged, forever known as the Luminari. Their very essence was forged from a fraction of the divine energy, creating a fusion of physical form and eternal spirit.

However, the remnants of this titanic clash birthed a single entity, a Luminari now referred to as the Prisoner. Composed of the residual energies that defied conventional comprehension, the Prisoner shared the same essence as its brethren but carried a different destiny.

In this new existence, the Luminari wielded free will, traversing planes with bodies and souls intertwined, commanding powers of both creation and destruction. Yet, the Prisoner foresaw the perils that this newfound autonomy could unleash upon the multiverse. In an act of unity, the Prisoner implored its kin to bind their powers, to mend the fissures wrought by their individual desires and prevent the looming conflicts that would ensue.

However, a chorus of dissent echoed among the Luminari. They reveled in their newfound ambitions, desires, and the blossoming selfishness that sprouted within their hearts. The Prisoner's vision of unity and harmony was misconstrued as an attempt to subjugate their individuality, and the Luminari found a common cause in labeling the Prisoner as the enemy.

A catastrophic clash erupted as these nascent beings unleashed their yet untamed powers, rending the fabric of reality with devastating force. The multitude of lesser-powered Luminari found momentary victories against the Prisoner, but the ever-growing wellspring of its power promised an imminent and inevitable reversal of fortune.

Amidst the tumultuous conflict, the Prisoner, fueled by an alternative path forced upon it, saw an opportunity in the face of overwhelming power. Instead of mending the schism and restoring harmony, the Prisoner embraced a dark resolve. It sought to consume and absorb the essence of its fellow Luminari, extinguishing their existence and augmenting its own power.

Recognizing the imminent threat posed by the Prisoner, the Luminari rallied, organizing their forces with a shared understanding of the cataclysmic consequences should they fail to halt its relentless advance. Their purpose crystallized as they embarked on a momentous quest to confine the Prisoner, utilizing their combined might and esoteric knowledge to erect an arcanite prison of immense strength.

This arcanite prison, woven from the very essence that empowered the Luminari, became a sentinel against the encroaching chaos. It served as both fortress and shackles, containing the boundless might of the Prisoner within its ethereal confines.

Thus, the silent history of Lumina unfolds, obscured by veils of secrecy and shrouded in unspoken truths. The Luminari's relentless pursuit to imprison the enigmatic Prisoner stands as a testament to their unwavering resolve and the enduring spirit of Lumina. Through their collective will and sacrifices, they succeeded in erecting a barrier of unparalleled strength, forever binding the Prisoner within the arcanite prison.

However, this victory came at a great cost. The energies harnessed to construct the prison were drawn from the very essence of the Luminari themselves, depleting their once boundless power. The act of containment, though noble and necessary, had forever altered the delicate balance of forces within Lumina.

With the Prisoner confined, the world of Lumina experienced a semblance of stability. Yet, the echoes of the conflict that had ravaged the land still reverberated through the annals of time. The scars left behind were not solely physical but etched into the hearts and minds of the Luminari, forever shaping their perspectives and guiding their actions.

The history of Lumina, veiled in silence and the weight of unspoken truths, continued to unfold. The legacy of the Luminari endured, their powers diminished but their spirit indomitable. They became the guardians of the arcanite prison, entrusted with the eternal vigilance to prevent the Prisoner's escape and the calamity it would unleash upon the realms.

Yet, as Lumina evolved and new generations emerged, the knowledge of the Prisoner and the sacrifices made by the Luminari began to fade. The lore that once bound them together grew fragmented, fragmented into fragments scattered across time, and the unspoken history slipped into obscurity.

Now, the destiny of Lumina lies in the hands of those who dare to uncover the secrets buried within its ancient past. As the forces of creation and destruction continue to ebb and flow, a new chapter in the cosmic dance unfolds. It is a chapter that calls for heroes to rise, for ancient prophecies to be fulfilled, and for the enigmatic Prisoner to either find redemption or unleash oblivion.

In this unspoken history, the whispered truths and forgotten knowledge hold the key to the salvation or demise of Lumina. As the world teeters on the edge of uncertainty, the time has come for the next generation to embrace their destinies, to illuminate the darkness, and to rewrite the tale of Lumina with the indomitable spirit that flows through their veins.

The story remains untold, awaiting the courageous souls who will breathe life into the forgotten lore, and guide Lumina toward its ultimate fate.

1. Aeons:

Introduction to Aeons and the Pleroma:

Aeons are celestial beings that exist within the context of the multiverse. They represent fundamental aspects of existence, embodying concepts, emotions, and cosmic forces. The Aeons are intricately connected to the cosmic balance, maintaining harmony and order throughout the realms.

The concept of Aeons draws inspiration from the Pleroma, an ancient philosophical and metaphysical term. In this context, the Pleroma refers to the totality of all divine powers and principles in existence, representing the fullness and completeness of the cosmos.

Aeons serve as guardians of the multiverse, ensuring that Creation and Oblivion are kept in balance. They act as cosmic regulators, preventing any one force from overpowering the other. The delicate equilibrium maintained by the Aeons is crucial for the stability and existence of all realms.

However, a significant event has occurred that has shattered the balance. The details of this event are not fully disclosed in the provided information, but it is implied that the Aeon responsible for maintaining balance on the planet Lumia was shattered, leading to chaos and upheaval.

The shattered Aeon, referred to as the Luminari, comprises various aspects and perspectives of a "perfect" being. Each Luminari, including the enigmatic figure known as the prisoner, represents a different facet of the original Aeon. While the prisoner is not inherently evil, it embodies the essence of nothingness and seeks Oblivion as a means of restoring balance.

The Luminari's power, referred to as their essence or souls, is intertwined with magic in the world. Arcanite, a substance closely associated with the Luminari and their prison, holds their power and fuels magic. Originally, the Luminari used a portion of their power to create the arcanite prison, which brought magic into the world.

However, as time passed and arcanite was depleted, the prison weakened and cracked, presenting an opportunity for the prisoner to pursue its goal of bringing about Oblivion. The prisoner manipulates and tailors its persuasive tactics based on the desires of those it targets.

Meanwhile, on Lumia, there was a primitive human civilization before the arrival of advanced beings like the Luminari. The shattered balance has drastically affected the planet, leaving it in a state of turmoil and uncertainty.

The crystal dwarves, a race with a deep connection to arcanite, possess knowledge about this mystical substance. Their expertise and unique culture make them vital players in understanding and potentially repairing the fractured balance.

In summary, the concept of Aeons introduces celestial beings responsible for upholding the cosmic balance. The shattered Luminari, including the prisoner, seeks to restore balance through Oblivion. Their power, linked to arcanite, fuels magic in the world. The crystal dwarves and their connection to arcanite hold crucial information. The planet Lumia has been profoundly affected by the shattered balance, leading to chaos and conflict.

It is important to strike a balance between providing a structured storyline that drives the narrative forward and allowing players the freedom to explore and make their mark on the world. A combination of overarching story arcs and player-driven content ensures a rich and dynamic narrative experience that accommodates both the narrative's progression and player agency.
