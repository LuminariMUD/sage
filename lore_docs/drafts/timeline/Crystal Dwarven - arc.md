Long before the last war a clan of dwarves settled in the caverns of what were then known as Crystal Caves. These caverns were lined with a strange, glowing magical crystal that would, in later times, come to be known as Arcanite.

When the power of the Arcanite became known, the dwarves coveted their hoard, closing and barring the doors to their halls at the eruption of the last war and in their mind, the end of the world above.

In their self-imposed isolation, the dwarves began to see a change in their physiology - Their skin hardening and becoming transparent, their minds sharpening and they lost the need to eat and drink. Over the years, they transformed in both body and mind, becoming the crystal dwarves of today. Seeing this as an evolution of their race and a step towards perfection, in general the dwarves embraced these changes.

Their culture evolved and soon the attainment of perfection of their bodies and mind became the foremost ideal. A caste system developed based on the purity of the crystals that compose them; those dwarves that had transparent bodies were deemed as nearing perfection while any hint of impurity would move one down into the lower castes. At the bottom of the caste system, the dwarves would have large swaths of minerals swirling through their crystalline forms, their bodies dark and mottled with various colors and textures. In contrast, those considered to be nobility have nearly completely transparent bodies, perhaps with a tint of color or a fleck of a precious metal.

Clothing is generally shunned, seen as something of the past, although the dwarves are very self-conscious of their perceived impurities and the lower castes cover themselves with gilding and woven metal fabrics.

Crystal dwarves are magical beings, closely tied to the arcanite from which they were born. At the center of their body is a node of crystalline arcanite, arranged into a delicate matrix. The glow from this 'heart' is visible through the dwarves bodies, although they can stifle the light if they so wish. If a crystal dwarf leaves the magical field generated by the arcanite on Lumina, their heart will slowly dim as it uses its stored energy to keep the dwarf alive. Once exhausted of energy, the crystal dwarf will die, its life force depleted. The heart of a crystal dwarf can not be used in the same way as other forms of crystalline arcanite due to the different structure.

The Crystalline Dwarves were in isolation from the time of the last war until about 80 years ago. As children and adults read and listen about the myth and the legends of crystal dwarves and how turned their bodies to entirely to crystal. Until a small group of dwarves rediscovered the true Crystalline Dwarves, making the myths and legends true. As relics of the elder times, the crystalline dwarves of Nagburim have become well-known among the surface races. Their records from that time are both a boon and a curse to the current generation.

Crystal dwarves keep a meticulous records and they have the only known information about the magical wonders of the time before the war - They also have records of the darkling and luminari artifacts that were used during the war, including the original arcana golems. From them came the ability to create the much lesser version that exist in Lumina today, partially based on the study of crystal dwarf physiology.

The dwarves possess the innate ability to sense the fields that created by crystalline arcanite, so it is much easier for them to find deposits. They have used this ability to establish themselves as the primary supplier of mined and refined arcanite to the surface races. Wary of potential risks of arcanite depletion to their entire race, they crystal dwarves are very careful to limit the mining of arcanite and will aggressively and without prejudice seek out and stop those that encroach on their monopoly.

The language of the crystal dwarves used is called Tal in the common tongue. They use vibrations/sounds of music to speak to each other. To non crystal dwarf, their speech sounds like a symphony of harmonic tones.
