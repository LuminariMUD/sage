Faction Overview

What are factions?

Factions are a mechanic tied to clans. The aim of factions will be to tie players together into social organisations which are working towards a shared goal. Events should be faction based. Ideally, we can have a staff member assigned to each NPC faction leader who will be able to spend some time in-character listening to players and taking their advice on, and there should be at least a few mini-events and maybe infrequent taking control of these mobs to keep them relevant. 

My plan for faction development based on discussion will be: 

Factions start small, they have specific goals and are very small scale. Over time, a bit of mystery will unravel, there should be a bit of drama and shuffles in identity, but the leadership should remain relatively fixed as an icon that represents the group. As time goes on, the starter factions should become more and more distant from their roots, their horizons and vision broadening as the plot develops.

Players should come to be respected in their factions and gain titles, (prefixes rather than suffixes) but these should never be outright LEADERSHIP roles. 

The discovery of Arcanite deposits could be a great hook into starting to develop the various factions. This could be the basis of faction base expansion and most other faction based mechanics. 

Factions should be multifaceted and cater to various players. They should be a vehicle for roleplay, combat, getting gear, getting buffs, finding new mechanics and uncovering the lore and histories of the world.

Bear in mind the setting: The world Luminari is set in has been devastated by war, magic and dark powers. While not exactly post-apocalyptic, the world is still rebuilding, civilization is recovering and in my mind, this should be reflected as a key theme.

There are plenty of ways to cause conflict between factions, but they should be able to acknowledge one another and inevitably be able to work together in some of the more dire scenarios. It would be good to create situations in which all the factions are pointing fingers at the others.

A fourth faction should emerge eventually to represent the darklings. How players will fit into this faction might be a discussion for another time, but it isn’t going to be one of the initial factions that people are going to be able to join, and the first appearances of this fourth faction should be antagonistic. Players should only be able to defect after this group appears a few times.

The Factions

The Mosswood Bandits (CN)

Valariella Woodthimble

“The law doesn’t protect those who need protecting the most… It couldn’t protect my husband!”

The Mosswood Bandits are led by a powerful rebel leader called Valariella, a ruthless half-elf ranger who spends her days training the common folk to protect themselves from threats, and turning them against the establishments that she claims only uphold the law when it comes to the rich and powerful. 

Her grudge against the village stems from the loss of her husband, who fell to a necromancer. For now, people are turning a blind eye to her activities as a sign of respect, but as she grows bolder, it is becoming harder for them to accept this.

Event Opportunity - Valariella is cast out of Mosswood by the Village Elder, who confronts her about her actions. She is forced to move out of her home, and sets up in the necromancer’s tower, clearing it out first. Players will help with clearing out the undead and making the place habitable.

Event Opportunity - Valariella is influenced by the dark magic of the place, and the still-beating heart of the necromancer she had killed. The actions of players will determine the long-term impact on her psyche and moral outlook, but she will fight the influence of the dark magic and win.

Event Opportunity - Valariella strikes out against Mosswood and Ashenport. The long-term consequences and how far she is willing to go will be determined by how players acted in the last phase. Expect retaliation.

Interest in Arcanite

Valariella plans to use Arcanite as a resource to create a place of safety for all. It will be used to vastly expand the necromancer’s tower. Her interest in Arcanite might take a slightly darker turn depending on previous phases, but she will never go full “dark side” and should remain a leader who can be respected.

The Ashenport Guards (LN)

Captain Halden Alfarth

“Our duty is clear, we must protect those who abide by the law, and punish those who don’t.”

The Ashenport Guards are led by an honourable Captain named Halden, a proud human warrior who has dedicated his whole life to the guard, following the tradition of many generations of his family. He trains up new guards and is known for his fair and just rulings. He is good at heart, although his fervour for the law can sometimes cloud his judgement.

His loyalties are tested daily by a tyrant ruler. Forced to watch as the powerful break laws with impunity, his unwavering faith in the guard is starting to fail, and perhaps it is time for a new guard to rise.

Event Opportunity - As some of the newer guards abuse their power over the citizenry, Halden is forced to act, and doubts about the ancient institution begin to creep in. 

Event Opportunity - Halden will set out and challenge some of those who have been abusing power at a higher level, confronting his tyrannical liege and getting into an argument. This will lead to Halden breaking the sword held gifted to his family many years ago, permanently marring the room. Depending on choices in previous phases, and whether he was advised to follow his heart over the law, he may become a paladin, and this will lead to a far more dramatic scene.

Event Opportunity - After being forced to protect his people from an attack without the resources and power he once held, Halden takes the chance to claim an old fortress which once belonged to his family and start building a formidable army. His paladin form will be far more humble and forgiving.

Interest in Arcanite

Halden has little interest in Arcanite past how he can use it to defend his people. He may choose to invest it in his fortress to bolster defences. He might begin to investigate Arcanite a little more from a traditional angle, perhaps stumbling upon Lady Serane and choosing to align with her. Though this will be a much later event, and Halden will remain the leader of the faction.

The Explorer's League (TN) 

Professor Filindel

“There are so many secrets to be unravelled, maps to be made, histories to be uncovered!”

The Explorer’s League is directed by a knowledgeable Professor and archivist who goes by Filindel, an ancient lorekeeper elven wizard with a passion for discovering new things. Along with his extremely intelligent familiar and assistant, he seeks to discover more about the lost history of the Luminari and the cataclysm that left the world in the state it was in. While not callous, his personality and elven upbringing can make him come across as slightly apathetic sometimes, and he has few moral qualms.

The fate of his assistant and archives will wreak havoc on the perfectly curated lifestyle he had lead up until this point, and spurred into action, he may find himself making difficult choices in the quest to protect and conserve knowledge, or uncover forbidden secrets best left alone.

Event Opportunity - Missing a few scrolls and maps, the professor may ask some players to go and gather them for him. This will involve searching areas and using special cartography tools provided by the elf. The discoveries made, or not made, may place his valued assistant in some danger.

Event Opportunity - As the archives come under threat from outside forces, the league are forced to upgrade some of their defences using techniques and methods found in the archives. However, an accident due to uncovering too much, or not discovering enough, could spell a nasty end for his assistant. Even if this event goes perfectly, something should happen. Having her attached to the location, or a location forever would be interesting.

Event Opportunity - Horrified, in response to his assistant’s fate and another attack on the archives, the professor stumbles across a text about the Arcana Golems. His temperament will shape how he reacts to this.

Interest in Arcanite

The professor cares for arcanite for entirely academic purposes at first, deeply interested in the histories regarding it, and at first, acknowledging the warnings. As time goes on though, his obsession goes beyond just protecting the archives, and the power becomes alluring.
