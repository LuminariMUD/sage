# The Treasures of Luminari
*A Chronicle of Wealth, Wonder, and the Weight of Power*

> "Every treasure tells three stories: how it was made, why it was lost, and what it costs to claim it. The wise adventurer learns all three before drawing blade or loosing spell."
> — Keeper Theron of the Ashenport Vaults

## The Nature of Treasure

In the world of Luminari, treasure is more than mere wealth—it is crystallized story, frozen possibility, the very threads of the Loom made manifest in metal and stone. Every coin bears the weight of its history, every blade remembers its forging, and every artifact dreams of the hands that will wield it.

The sages speak of treasure's three aspects, echoing the tripartite nature that governs all things in this realm:

- **Memory** (what was): The history embedded in each item
- **Purpose** (what is): The power it grants in the present moment
- **Destiny** (what will be): The fate it draws toward its bearer

## The Hierarchy of Wonder

### Mundane Treasures (Levels 1-6)
*The Foundation of Fortune*

These are the coppers and silvers that oil the gears of civilization, the simple steel that guards merchant caravans, the rough-spun cloaks that turn autumn rain. Yet even these humble items may hide significance—many a legendary blade began as a soldier's trusty sword, many a crown started as a merchant's lucky coin.

**Progression Formula**: Level × 10 gold pieces
- A beginning adventurer's purse: 10-60 gold
- Enhancement bonuses: +0 to +1
- Simple resistances: 5-10% against common elements

**Notable Categories**:
- **Traveler's Gear**: Basic armor and weapons, often bearing maker's marks from Ashenport forges or Mosswood workshops
- **Provisions**: Foods and drinks that grant brief respite (+1 to attributes, 20-50 ticks duration)
- **Components**: Raw materials for crafting—iron from the Memlin Caverns, leather from the Quagmire beasts

### Exceptional Treasures (Levels 7-12)
*Where Magic Begins to Whisper*

At this tier, items begin to resonate with the Loom itself. A sword might hum when drawn, armor might warm against coming danger, a ring might tighten when lies are spoken nearby. These are not yet true magic items, but they stand at the threshold.

**Progression Formula**: Level² × 75 experience value
- An established adventurer's hoard: 70-120 gold per venture
- Enhancement bonuses: +2 to +3
- Moderate resistances: 15-25% against uncommon damage types

**Notable Categories**:
- **Quenched Steel**: Weapons tempered in the springs of Graven Hollow, naturally resistant to rust and wear
- **Woven Wards**: Garments threaded with silver from the Spider Swamp, offering protection against minor enchantments
- **Alchemical Provisions**: Enhanced foods and drinks (600 tick duration, +2 attributes, 1000 gold value)

### Mystical Treasures (Levels 13-18)
*The First Touch of True Power*

Here begin the items of story and song. Each bears a name, each has a tale. The Knights of the Sacred Orders carry weapons of this caliber, and merchant princes guard such treasures in vaults of solid stone. These items remember their wielders and may show loyalty—or judgment.

**Progression Formula**: Enhancement = Level/6 + Rarity modifier
- A hero's ransom: 130-180 gold per quest
- Enhancement bonuses: +4 to +5
- Strong resistances: 30-40% against rare damage types
- First manifestations of special abilities

**The Forging Traditions**:
- **Ember Throne Technique**: Each knight forges their own weapon in divine fire, tempering it with fears and hopes
- **Crystal Dwarf Crafting**: Items grown rather than forged, incorporating living crystal that responds to intent
- **Arcana Golem Integration**: Soul-vessels that can bond with items, granting them limited sentience

### Legendary Treasures (Levels 19-24)
*Echoes of the Ages*

These items have shaped kingdoms, ended bloodlines, and started wars. They are known by name across the continents, and their histories fill entire volumes in the libraries of Sanctus. To bear such an item is to carry a fragment of history itself.

**Progression Formula**: Maximum enhancement approaches +8 to +10
- A kingdom's treasury: 190-240 gold per epic quest
- Multiple resistance types: 45-60% against various threats
- Active magical abilities: Procs, spell-casting, transformation

**The Sacred Weapons**:
- **Oathblades**: Weapons of the Crown Knights, forged from crystallized oaths, growing stronger with each fulfilled vow
- **The Twin Blades of Eclipse**: Mercy's Edge (drawn only in light) and Necessity's Bite (drawn only in darkness)
- **Phoenixwrought Arms**: Weapons reforged through destruction, each breaking making them stronger

### Artifacts of Power (Levels 25-30+)
*Threads That Bind Reality*

These are not mere items but forces of nature given form. Each artifact is unique, irreplaceable, and dangerous to both wielder and world. They exist at the intersection of all three aspects of the Loom—Memory, Will, and Possibility—and their use can reshape the very fabric of reality.

**Progression Formula**: Enhancement +11 to +12 (theoretical maximum)
- Beyond mere gold: Requires epic quests, divine favor, or terrible sacrifice
- Near-immunity resistances: 70-90% against specific threats
- World-shaping abilities: Reality alteration, divine intervention, planar travel

**Known Artifacts** (rumored to exist):
- **The Five Locks**: Items of binding that hold the Prisoner—their location scattered and secret
- **Serane's Burden**: The last Luminari's weapon, said to cut through fate itself
- **The Arcanite Heart**: A prototype arcana golem core, the closest anyone has come to recreating the mighty constructs of old
- **The Loom-Threader**: A needle that can repair—or unravel—the fabric of reality

## The Arcanite Progression

*"We mine tomorrow to forge today's power, but what happens when tomorrow runs dry?"*
— Master Smith Thorkin, before the Sealing of Nagburim

### Understanding Arcanite

Arcanite is crystallized possibility, formed where the Primal Realm touched our world. It exists in multiple states:

1. **Raw Arcanite**: Chaotic, dangerous, found deep beneath the earth
2. **Refined Arcanite**: Stabilized for crafting, the foundation of enchantment
3. **Living Arcanite**: The hearts of Crystal Dwarves, too complex to harvest
4. **Corrupted Arcanite**: Touched by the Prisoner, grants power at terrible cost

### The Enhancement Ladder

Items progress through Arcanite infusion in careful stages:

**Stage 1 - Preparation** (Levels 1-6)
- Item is prepared with alchemical treatments
- Micro-channels carved for Arcanite flow
- Initial resonance established

**Stage 2 - Infusion** (Levels 7-12)
- Raw Arcanite dust worked into the item's structure
- Basic enhancement bonuses manifest
- Item begins to "remember" its purpose

**Stage 3 - Awakening** (Levels 13-18)
- Refined Arcanite crystals embedded at key points
- Item develops personality traits
- Special abilities begin to emerge

**Stage 4 - Transcendence** (Levels 19-24)
- Item partially exists in multiple realms
- Can channel raw magical energies
- Develops autonomous protective abilities

**Stage 5 - Apotheosis** (Levels 25-30+)
- Item becomes a node in the Loom itself
- Reality bends around its presence
- May develop consciousness and will

### The Arcanite Paradox

Every piece of Arcanite mined weakens the barrier between realms. The Crystal Dwarves know this truth: we are consuming our prison walls for power. Yet without Arcanite, civilization would collapse back to the Age of Shadows. This is the balance that must be maintained—enough mining to sustain progress, not so much that we invite catastrophe.

## The Art of Crafting

### The Three Schools of Making

#### 1. The Forge Tradition
*Metal and Fire, Will and Desire*

Practiced primarily by the Knights of the Ember Throne and the smiths of Mithril Hall. This tradition sees crafting as transformation—taking base materials and elevating them through fire, force, and intent.

**Core Principles**:
- Every strike of the hammer is a prayer
- The smith's emotion transfers to the blade
- Impurities burned away reveal truth

**Mechanical Framework**:
- Requires: Smithing skill, forge access, metal materials
- Produces: Weapons, armor, shields
- Special: Can incorporate memories into items during forging

#### 2. The Growth Tradition
*Crystal and Resonance, Patience and Harmony*

The Crystal Dwarves' contribution to crafting lore. Items are not made but grown, guided from seed to fruition through careful cultivation of crystalline structures.

**Core Principles**:
- Every crystal has an optimal form waiting to emerge
- The crafter guides, never forces
- Time is an ingredient as vital as material

**Mechanical Framework**:
- Requires: Crystal singing skill, growth chambers, seed crystals
- Produces: Jewelry, focuses, resonance items
- Special: Items continue to evolve with use

#### 3. The Binding Tradition
*Soul and Vessel, Contract and Consequence*

The most dangerous path, practiced by those who understand that the greatest power comes from sacrifice. Arcana Golems exemplify this tradition—souls bound to constructed forms.

**Core Principles**:
- Every binding requires consent (willing or coerced)
- Power drawn from sacrifice cannot be undone
- The vessel shapes the soul, the soul shapes the vessel

**Mechanical Framework**:
- Requires: Soul binding skill, prepared vessel, willing essence
- Produces: Sentient items, golems, cursed objects
- Special: Items may act independently or rebel

### Materials of Wonder

#### Common Crafting Components (Levels 1-10)
- **Iron**: The foundation, honest and strong
- **Silver**: Naturally opposed to shadow and corruption
- **Gold**: Conducts magical energy without resistance
- **Leather**: From beasts of the wild, retains primal essence
- **Wood**: Living memory of the forests

#### Uncommon Materials (Levels 11-20)
- **Mithril**: Light as silk, strong as dragon scale
- **Adamantine**: Unbreaking, but difficult to work
- **Moonstone**: Phases between material and ethereal
- **Dragonhide**: Retains elemental resistances
- **Starwood**: Fallen from the heavens, naturally magical

#### Rare Essences (Levels 21-30)
- **Crystallized Oath**: Formed from kept promises
- **Shadow Silk**: Woven from darkness itself
- **Phoenix Ash**: Death and rebirth in powder form
- **Void Stone**: Absence given weight
- **Dream Amber**: Crystallized possibility

#### Legendary Substances (Artifacts)
- **Pure Arcanite**: Possibility incarnate
- **God-blood**: Divine essence made material
- **First Iron**: Metal from the world's creation
- **The Prisoner's Touch**: Corruption crystallized
- **Loom Thread**: Reality's fundamental fiber

## Treasures Across the Realm

### The Continental Vaults

Each region of Luminari has developed its own treasure traditions:

#### Continent 1 - The Heartlands
*Where Adventure Begins*

Home to Ashenport, Mosswood, and the great trade routes. Treasures here tend toward the practical—reliable steel, proven enchantments, time-tested designs.

**Signature Items**:
- Ashenport Trade Bars (standardized currency)
- Mosswood Cloaks (weather resistance)
- Caravan Guard Steel (durability focused)

#### Continent 5 - The Eastern Mysteries
*Sanctus and the Exotic Markets*

The great city of Sanctus serves as a hub for exotic treasures from across the planes. Items here often blur the line between treasure and art.

**Signature Items**:
- Sanctus Meditation Crystals (mental resistances)
- Glass Tower Focuses (spell enhancement)
- Eastern Silk Armor (maximum dexterity)

#### Continent 8 - The Frozen Reaches
*Where Ice Preserves Ancient Power*

The Frozen Castle and Zhentil Keep guard treasures from before the last age. Items found here often carry the weight of ancient history.

**Signature Items**:
- Frozen Castle Ice-weapons (cold damage)
- Ancient Preservation Vessels (time-locked items)
- Bloodfist Brutal-gear (raw damage focus)

## The Weight of Wealth

### The Slot System of Power

Not all who seek power can bear its weight. The body can only channel so much magical energy before burning out. Thus, the ancient limitation:

**Power Distribution by Body Location**:
- **Head** (18.75%): Crowns of command, helms of wisdom
- **Body** (43.75%): The core, where life force gathers
- **Arms** (18.75%): Strength and skill enhanced
- **Legs** (18.75%): Speed and stability
- **Fingers** (Dual): Two rings maximum, opposing forces
- **Neck**: The throat of power, voice and will
- **Waist**: The center, where energies balance
- **Wrists**: Pulse points, where magic enters blood

**The Resistance Web**:
Each slot specializes in specific protections:
- Physical resistances (slice, puncture, impact)
- Elemental resistances (fire, cold, electricity, acid)
- Planar resistances (holy, unholy, shadow, light)
- Mental resistances (charm, fear, madness)

### Food and Drink - The Temporary Transcendence

*"Eat of my table, drink of my cup, and for this moment, you shall know the strength of gods."*
— The Last Feast of King Alerion

Where permanent items change who you are, consumables change who you are *right now*. The traditions of enhancement through consumption run deep:

**Common Fare** (10-100 gold)
- Traveler's Bread: +1 Constitution, 20 ticks
- Warrior's Drought: +1 Strength, 30 ticks
- Scout's Berries: +1 Dexterity, 25 ticks

**Exceptional Cuisine** (1000 gold)
- Dragon Pepper Steak: +2 all physical stats, 600 ticks
- Moonwell Water: +2 all mental stats, 600 ticks
- Phoenix Egg Omelet: Regeneration 5 hp/tick, 600 ticks

**Legendary Banquets** (Priceless)
- Ambrosia of the Gods: Temporary immortality
- The Prisoner's Cup: Power beyond measure, corruption guaranteed
- Loom-Threader Tea: Perceive fate itself

## The Economics of Adventure

### The Accumulation Formula

Wealth follows predictable patterns, yet each adventurer's path diverges:

**Level 1-10**: Building the Foundation
- Average wealth: 10-100 gold
- Focus: Basic equipment, consumables
- Goal: Survival and establishment

**Level 11-20**: The Ascending Path
- Average wealth: 110-200 gold
- Focus: Specialized gear, resistance stacking
- Goal: Reputation and influence

**Level 21-30**: The Legendary Threshold
- Average wealth: 210-300 gold (currency becomes less relevant)
- Focus: Artifacts, favors, cosmic significance
- Goal: Shaping the world itself

### Quest Point Economy

Beyond gold lies a deeper currency—the favor of fate itself:

**Formula**: 1 + (level² ÷ 5) + (10 × level)

Quest points represent completed stories, fulfilled destinies, threads properly woven into the Loom. They can purchase:
- Bazaar mysteries (items that shouldn't exist)
- Divine favors (temporary blessings)
- Fate reversals (second chances)
- Planar passages (doors that shouldn't open)

## The Crafting Renaissance

### Current State - The Sleeping Giant

Crafting in Luminari currently slumbers, its potential untapped:

**The Problems**:
- Crafted items lack end-game viability
- Materials are scarce and poorly distributed
- The grind overshadows the reward
- No connection to greater lore or purpose

**The Proposed Awakening**:

1. **Enable Full Enchantment**: Crafters should access all possible item properties
2. **Establish Material Hierarchies**: From common iron to legendary Arcanite
3. **Create Meaningful Progression**: Each crafting level opens new possibilities
4. **Connect to Lore**: Crafting traditions tied to cultures and histories
5. **Risk and Reward**: Powerful crafting requires dangerous material gathering

### The Future of Making

Three paths forward for crafting's evolution:

#### Path of the Questsmith
- Crafting quests that tell stories
- Materials gathered through adventure
- Each crafted item has personal significance

#### Path of the Guildmaster
- Collaborative crafting requiring multiple artisans
- Guild resources and shared knowledge
- Prestige items only possible through cooperation

#### Path of the Innovator
- Experimental crafting with risk of failure
- Discovery of new techniques and combinations
- Possibility of creating entirely new item types

## The Philosophy of Treasure

### Why We Seek

Every treasure seeker follows one of three paths, though they may not know it:

1. **Memory Seekers**: Hunt treasures for their stories
2. **Power Seekers**: Pursue strength regardless of cost
3. **Destiny Seekers**: Believe certain items are meant for them

### The Cost of Power

Every treasure exacts a price:

- **Mundane**: Gold and effort
- **Magical**: Attunement and energy
- **Legendary**: Responsibility and consequence
- **Artifact**: Soul and sanity

### The Ultimate Question

In the end, every treasure asks the same question: "What are you willing to sacrifice for power?"

The wise adventurer knows that the greatest treasures are not those that make you powerful, but those that make you whole. The Loom cares nothing for your strength—it remembers only whether you wove your thread with purpose or let it tangle in greed.
