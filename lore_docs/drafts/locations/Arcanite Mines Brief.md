# Arcanite Mines Brief

*Converted from: Arcanite Mines Brief.docx*

Area Brief - Luminari Mud

Name:The Arcanite Mines

Description:Darkling Cultists have stumbled across a rich site of Arcanite and are mining to use in their rituals, Infiltrate the mines and find your way through the Overseer to put an end to this dangerous operation.

Level range: 25 - 30

Purpose:A mine zone, multiple layers, small passageways, dead ends, needs mapping / exploring etc.  Some slightly challenging mobs, with a Boss mob in the Cult Leader and a treasure chest (level 30)

Size:Medium (30 - 60 Rooms)

Location:Linked directly off the map, in a Mountain range with not much else going on.

Zone make up:

4 Levels each with a mini boss and a locked door to get through on level 1 - 3.

Average to Rare drops on Mini Bosses - perhaps crafting crystals to encourage crafting (it is a mine after all.)

Level 1 - Giant Arcanite infused rats (26), 

 - Mini Boss is a Larger version (28) heal potion drop

Level 2 - Arcanite Twisted Miners Humanoids, Use class skills , Extra Gold Drops (28)

 - Overseer (30) Weapon Crystal Enchantment drop

Level 3 - Arcanite Golems(30), Physical resistance, extra HP,  low % crafting crystals

- Greater Arcanite Golem (32) - Extra Feat Crystal drop

Level 4 - Darkling Cultist (32), Spell casters, stone skin, low % spell slot crystals

- Darkling Cult Leader (35) Spell caster, Epic Ward, good % spell slot crystal + random Loot chest
