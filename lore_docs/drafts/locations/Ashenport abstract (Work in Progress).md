Ashenport

The Great Port of Lumia

(Will put more in when given more information on landmarks and areas surrounding Ashenport)

Ashenport is the most highly populated metropolis, found in the center of Lumia. Nearly every being that can speak has heard of it, and it’s likely every adventurer, merchant, and sailor has been inside it’s walls. Anything can be found there, for better or worse, and everything is in a pseudo-balance.

The government is quite there could be in Lumia. There is a Mayor of the city, but he only conducts and deals with internal affairs within the city, as well as appointing judges and the guard. The real governmental power is a republic that controls the lands and seas around Ashenport, as well as military. It is a council of nine members, consisting of four nobles and five elected commoners, with a tie-breaking vote given to the Field Marshal of the military if an odd amount of chancellors abstain. This is much more stable than outsiders believe, since the nobles and commoners rarely agree, and there is always some sort of infighting.

Despite the government situation being stable, the city tends to be alight with commerce, parties, rumors, and crime. The port area has taverns with hearty stew and boisterous stories, the alleys have beggars and bruisers looking for coin. The bazaar has merchants looking to make a sale, and the streets have kids and housewives with the latest gossip. There is always something to do, and always something to see.

The population mainly consists of humans, comprising a little over half the total population. Elves come and go often, and a few halflings, gnomes, and other races make home here, even if only waiting in port.

Lately rumors have been spreading about more aggressive creatures along the borders that Ashenport controls. Most merchants see this as a hindrance, and wares will be sparse until these threats have ceased.
