Crystal Dwarves

The Crystalkin

Inspiration gallery: http://www.galleries.com/minerals/property/crystal.htm

Home City: Nagburim

Location on Map:

Long before the last war a clan of dwarves settled in the caverns of what were then known as The Crystal Caves. These caverns were rich in a strange, glowing magical crystal that would come to be known as crystalline arcanite.

When the power of crystalline arcanite became known, the dwarves coveted their hoard, closing and barring the doors to their halls at the eruption of the last war and, in their mind, the end of the world above.

In their self-imposed isolation, the dwarves began to see a change in their physiology - Their skin hardened and became transparent, their minds sharpened and they lost the need to eat and drink. Over the years, they transformed in both body and mind, becoming the crystal dwarves of today. Seeing this as an evolution of their race and a step towards perfection the crystal dwarves embraced these changes.

Their culture evolved and soon the attainment of perfection of their bodies and mind became the foremost ideal. A caste system developed based on the purity of the crystals that compose them; those dwarves that had transparent bodies were deemed as nearing perfection while any hint of impurity would move one down into the lower castes. At the bottom of the caste system, the dwarves would have large swaths of minerals swirling through their crystalline forms, their bodies dark and mottled with various colors and textures. In contrast, those considered to be nobility have nearly completely transparent bodies, perhaps with a tint of color or a fleck of a precious metal.

Th crystal dwarf culture, clothing is generally shunned, seen as a relic of the past, although most crystal dwarves are very self-conscious of their perceived impurities and the lower castes often cover themselves with gilding and woven metal fabrics.

Those crystal dwarves convicted of heinous crimes, including crimes against their race, are often punished with magically enchanted adamantine spikes hammered into their bodies.  These spikes cause constant moderate pain for the crystal dwarf and allow the authorities to track them anywhere in the world.  With a command word, the spike causes paralysis and excruciating pain, effectively controlling the crystal dwarf for the rest of its existence.  Attempts to remove the device result in the instant and permanent death of the crystal dwarf.  As they are placed in obvious locations, such as the forehead, any crystal dwarves disfigured with these spikes can expect to be shunned and discriminated against in crystal dwarven society, if not outright assaulted.  Often, the recipients of these spikes become monks, relying on their learned focus and meditation to help control the constant pain that they must live with every day of their immortal existence.  

Crystal dwarves are magical beings, closely tied to the arcanite from which they were born. At the center of their body is a node of crystalline arcanite, arranged into a delicate matrix. The glow from this 'heart' is visible through the dwarves bodies, although they can stifle the light if they so wish. If a crystal dwarf leaves the magical field generated by the arcanite on Lumina, their heart will slowly dim as it uses its stored energy to keep the dwarf alive. Once exhausted of energy, the crystal dwarf will die, its life force depleted. The heart of a crystal dwarf can not be used in the same way as other forms of crystalline arcanite, although if it were possible to extract the crystal intact, containing the soul, it could have potential magical and alchemical uses.

The Crystalline Dwarves were in isolation from the time of the last war until about 80 years ago. As children and adults read and listen about the myth and the legends of crystal dwarves and how turned their bodies to entirely to crystal. Until a small group of dwarves rediscovered the true Crystalline Dwarves, making the myths and legends true. As relics of the elder times, the crystalline dwarves of Nagburim have become well-known among the surface races. Their records from that time are both a boon and a curse to the current generation.

Crystal dwarves keep meticulous records and they have the only known information about the magical wonders of the time before the war - They also have records of the darkling and luminari artifacts that were used during the war, including the original arcana golems. From them came the ability to create the much lesser version that exists in Lumina today, partially based on the study of crystal dwarf physiology.

The dwarves possess the innate ability to sense the fields created by crystalline arcanite, so it is much easier for them to find deposits of this valuable resource. They have used this ability to establish themselves as the primary supplier of raw and refined arcanite to the surface races. Wary of potential risks of arcanite depletion to their entire race, they crystal dwarves are very careful to control the mining of arcanite and will aggressively and without prejudice seek out and stop those that encroach on their monopoly.

Arcana Golems

The War-Machines

A re-engineering of artifacts from a by-gone age, arcana golems are considered by some to be just another step in the evolution of intelligent life.  Others consider them to be the embodiment of mortal hubris, an attempt to escape the certainty of death.  The product of powerful mages, each arcana golem is meticulously crafted into a vessel for a mortal soul.  It's body is usually created to resemble the type of soul that will occupy it, unlike the arcana golems of antiquity that were designed for war.  Newer golems are more versatile and can pass, at first glance, as the race they are built to resemble.  Inside, however, they could not possibly be more different.

The most important component in the construction of an Arcana Golem is the Soulgem - The vessel that contains the soul in control of the golem.  A Soulgem is a fist-sized semi-transparent crystal glowing with a faint light from within.  Careful examination reveals a swirling mist-like energy present deep within the gem.  If an arcana golem's Soulgem is destroyed, the golem ceases to function, similarly upon it's death or destruction, an arcana golem's Soulgem cracks and is rendered unusable.

Elves

The Children of the Pools

The Elves of Lumia are a people born of the Otherworld.  The ancestors of the elves, the ????????, originated in the Otherworld.  A group of these fey creatures had a desire to leave the otherworld, to gain the permenancy and solidity unknown in that shifting realm.  Unable to make the transition individually, they pooled their power and joined together into a single entity, piercing the feyveil and in doing so, drained themselves.  Seperating again into individuals, the pre-elves crossed into the physical realm, emerging from the Pools of Twilight as mortal elves, naked and with the minds of children.  Quickly learning the ways of their new world, the elves used their long lives and half-remembered, dream like memories of the otherworld to find their place on Lumia.  

The pools of Twilight exist still and their location is a closely guarded secret.  Old elves, tired of life and loss, can enter the pools.  What happens to them is unknown, but they never return.  

Half-Orcs

The Warrior-born

Origin - Desperately losing the battle against the horrors of the First Darkling Wars, the Anterian Alliance needed an edge.  They found their answer in a little-known region outside their borders, the Magocracy of Chulan.  The Chulanese sorcerers combined the strength and resiliency of the bestial Orcs with the intelligence and temperment of humans, providing the Alliance with soldiers physically far superior than their current army of conscripts.  These Half-Orcs,as they were called, became the new shock troops of the Alliance.  When paired with strong leaders, the half orc squads swept through the battlefields, pushing back the darkling hordes and finally raising hope for victory.

The remaining Half-Orcs, now released from their servitude with the dissolution of Old Anteria, are a people who lack a sense of purpose.  They have formed communities across Lumia, usually close knit bands of nomads who travel from town to town offering their services as mercenaries or bodyguards.  Generally mistrusted if not outright hated due to their bestial origins, they tend to settle some distance away from major communities, with small satellite camps nearer to population centres where those who wish to hire them can find them.

The Shadowmoon (Black Moon) - An astronomical object of unknown origin (potentially a moon) that 'bends' Lumia's magical field, causing magical tides and storms, weakening the prison and allowing the Unmaker to influence the world.  This object is on a very large orbit and only comes in near enough proximity every several thousands of years.  It is black in the sky, and only visible at night because of the way it blots out the stars.  When close, it is also visible during the day as a disc of pure blackness hanging in the sky.  It glows with an intense magical aura, visible to those who are sensitive to such things (detect magic) and it is plainly visible to those with darkvision or infravision.

Mosswood

The Forest Town

‘Ptah! This beer tastes like tree sap!.’ - Perigrin Tal, Mercenary

When a few disgruntled citizens left Ashenport, leaving the noise, dirty streets and their debt behind, they settled in the forest known as the Mosswood, to the northeast of the city.  Carving out a place in the forest, they built a town that has since attracted peoples from all walks of life.  Living in the forest and using its resources to limit their reliance on Ashenport, the people of Mosswood built up their meager settlement to a bustling town that is often used as a quick stop before, or an alternative to, the City of Ash.

Mosswood produces its own food, clearing areas of the forest and farming the fertile soil.  The trees are used for construction and even the sap is used to brew a kind of beer that has become popular in niche markets throughout New Anteria.  

Mosswood has a militia, but also employs mercenaries for removing dangerous monsters from the surrounding areas.  There are even rumors that a dragon has nested in the area, resulting in Mosswood becoming more visible to powerful factions.

Ashenport

The Great Port of Lumia, the Phoenix City, the City of Ash

‘Ashenport is both the oldest and the newest city in New Anteria - It’s bones run deep but most have no idea of the history of the the City of Ash.’ - Allthair Lenadrian, scholar and former adventurer

Ashenport is the most highly populated metropolis, and most maps are centered on this city. Nearly every being that can speak has heard of it, and it’s likely every adventurer, merchant, and sailor has been inside it’s walls. Anything can be found there, for better or worse, and everything is in a pseudo-balance.

Due to the stability of the region , or perhaps in spite of it, the city tends to be alight with commerce, parties, rumors, and crime. The port area has taverns with hearty stew and boisterous stories, the alleys have beggars and bruisers looking for coin. The bazaar has merchants looking to make a sale, and the streets have kids and housewives with the latest gossip.  The streets have the usual mix of peddlers, propagandists, prophets and prostitutes - sometimes combined within the same person.  There is always something to do, and always something to see.

The population mainly consists of humans, comprising a little over half the total population. Elves come and go often, and a few halflings, gnomes, and other races make home here, even if only waiting in port.  Crystal Dwarves are a rare sighting, as they often disguise themselves while roaming the streets.  Half-orcs are tolerated, although the public opinion of their kind tends to be rather low.  Half-trolls are met with severe skepticism, and additional guards are usually assigned to follow them through the city, at a healthy distance.

Lately rumors have been spreading about more aggressive creatures along the borders that Ashenport controls. Most merchants see this as a hindrance, and wares will be sparse until these threats have ceased.

People of Note:

The Lenadrian family

Althair Lenadrian (deceased) - The father, the explorer.

Eccentric and curious, Althair Lenadrian was an adventurer and explorer.  It was said that he set foot on every continent on Lumia - and left a bit of his sanity on every one.  In his early years, he served as a mercenary working for New Anteria, protecting the city of Ashenport from roving monsters and other dark things.  During one of his assignments he stumbled upon a cave some distance from the city.  Visiting this ancient place woke something in his mind, and he left his mercenary company to adventure and explore.

He came back to Ashenport in his later years with the spoils of his adventuring career, purchasing a manor and mostly keeping to himself.  It was in this period he met the love of his life, the Lady Cecilia Astorian, and started a family.  Their first born was Alerion, and Ulerion came into the world two years later.  Unfortunately, after the birth of Ulerion she fell mysteriously ill and simply whithered away, dying two years later.

After the death of his wife, Althair became driven, researching the history of Lumia and specifically the origin of the ruins and strange artifacts that are found nearly everywhere on and under its surface.  He raised his sons as best as he could, but his work came first and he relied on the family’s many servants to take care of their needs.  

As he grew increasingly older and more isolated, Althair seemed to focus more and more on the Luminari, the figures of legend and myth who were at one point in history considered to be gods of a sort.  Most of the history of that era were lost to time, destroyed after the last Darkling war, and his fascination became an obsession which consumed him in his later years.

In particular, an expedition to a site of interest not far from the city of Ashenport resulted int he loss of his team, either at the site in question or soon after.  Each member of the team that returned was quickly consumed by insanity and death.  Althair himself seemed to lose his mind, ranting and raving about creatures of smoke and oil, horrors from the past and a threat that loomed over all.  Dismissed as a raving madman, he spent the rest of his days in his manor with his son Ulerion and what few servants were loyal enough to remain.  His son Alerion was among those who considered the old man to be insane, a fact which pained the elder Lenadrian to then end of his days.

Cecilia Lenadrian (formerly Astorian, deceased) - The wife and mother, the flower that wilted.

As curious and kind as she was beautiful, Cecilia Lenadrian was the youngest daughter of the Astorian family.  She met the eccentric Althair Lenadrian when he purchased a lot of archaeological artifacts from her father.  She had catalogued the artifacts herself and her curiosity and intelligence were extremely attractive to the older man.

Alerion Lenadrian - The fallen, the prodigal son.

Former paladin, fallen.  Alcoholic.  Now attempting to redeem himself while also following in his father’s footsteps.

Ulerion Lenadrian - The son who remained.

Ulerion worked with his father, retrieving and studying history, magic and technology from the time of the Darkling wars.
