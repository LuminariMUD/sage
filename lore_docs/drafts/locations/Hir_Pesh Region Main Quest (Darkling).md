The Secret History

The part of history unspoken and unwritten is that we know that the one we call the Prisoner is actually of the same essence as the Luminari.

In the unfathomable dance of the “beginning,” the Infinite Eternal released agents of creation, known as the Aeon and agents of destruction, known as the Khadhu.  Agents which hand no will nor sentience outside of execution of their purpose.  Agents that transcended both the physical, extra-planar and spiritual dimensions.  Wishing to see where creation would go, the Infinite released a pulse of free will that caused “fissures” of various degrees in the various Aeon and Khadhu.

That’s where our story begins, through this fissuring one such Aeon - named Luminous, and a Khadhu - named Coshek collide.

The resulting explosion gave birth to a multitude of beings known as what we now call the Luminari.  A very small portion of this energy created the initial physical and eternal spiritual essence that makes up all of the Luminari that have existed.  All the remaining energy created a single Luminari which we now call the Prisoner.

Now existed these beings with free will, physical existence that traveled the planes, and (varied) powers of both creation and destruction.

The Prisoner saw that this would not be a good thing and that tremendous conflict would come from it.  He campaigned to the Luminari that they should unite their power to rejoin, remerge and fix the fissures to avoid the coming conflict.

But there was consensus among all the rest of the Luminari - they had their own newfound desires, ambitions and even selfishness that didn’t include merging.

What the Prisoner proposed as a big picture solution, was seen as an attempt to subjugate the Luminari from their newfound individuality.  And they found common cause identifying him as the enemy resulting in horrifically catastrophic conflict as these new beings wielded their yet unknown power.   It turned out the multitude of the much less powerful Luminari was enough to create repeated victories against the Prisoner, but as his ability to tap power grew they knew what would be coming.  The Prisoner, instead of mending the schism, saw a new path through the conflict pushed upon him and his growing power - he could just destroy them all and absorb their essence.

The Luminari in response became more organized and saw what would be if they could not stop the Prisoner, whose power had gradually started to dwarf theirs.  Thus began their mission to imprison him.
