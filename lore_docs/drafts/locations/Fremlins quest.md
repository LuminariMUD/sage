# Fremlins quest

*Converted from: Fremlins quest.docx*

Quest Name: 

The nicer step-child

Campaign

Questmaster

Zone

1452

Quest Type

informational

The main goal of the quest is to gain more information about the fremlins. Starting the quest will be at the mercenary camp in Mosswood, Southeast of the city. This quest will be multiple parts in gaining information about the fremlins.

Part 1: 

In this part, they need to find the location of the the fremlins. This will entail finding a group of fremlins.

Part 2:

A large green man ran off with a trinket of theirs and want it back. They don’t have any information about the green man except a description of him. No jolly green giant, it was a man in a ghillie suit. Either through intense questioning of the fremlins or a curious conversation a local that they will figure this out.

Part 3:

As one man in a ghillie suit is hard to find, they will have to smoke the stalker(hunter) out.This will lead him to attack or whether or not your weapons are out. If not this will trigger a conversation with him. Eventually, the players will find out that the fremlins were really annoying and the reason why the ghillied man stole one of their trinkets.

Part 4: 

Return the trinket to the fremlins and starts a trade with them, which you are given a braided friendship bracelet with an enchantment.
