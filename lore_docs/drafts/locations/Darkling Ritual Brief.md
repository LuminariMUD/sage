Area Brief - Luminari Mud

Name:The Darkling Ritual

Description:Following the “Death” of Raven the Necromancer, you have heard that a circle of Darklings are invoking a ritual to resurrect her, gather a party of 2 - 4 members between level 7 and 11 and stop this ritual taking place.

Level range: 7 - 11 (locked - via Quest / Trigger)

Purpose: A small dungeon, aimed at encouraging group play, including some basic mechanics to make the fight more interesting.  Rewards decent level 10 equipment / crafting crystals + Random Loot Container.

3 PhasesDungeon phase 1.)

4 rooms

1 player spawn (portal?)

3 in a line south of that room.

X

XXX

Central room has the entrance to next phase, and a very weak scout mob

2 on either side have stronger cultist mobs.

Phase one mechanic - greet trigger on scout to put up a barrier that stops you getting through to the main area. Can be avoided by sneaking in and killing him.

Once the barrier is up - you have to kill the two cultist mobs on either side to bring down the barrier. Rewards use of a sneak class to get around the mechanics, but doesn’t lock out brute force option

Then move to the main ritual site.

Dungeon 2.)

5 Rooms

1: Entrance to a Cave going underground

two Guards - nothing special about them accept maybe some dialogue

2: The Ritual Chamber with 2 rooms off it (3 and 4)

The Darkling Summoner is the main ‘boss’ who will freeze everybody on arrival for some dialogue about resurrecting Raven, and how they happily sacrifice their lives to be part of the Darkness.

Combat phase 1 - Standard fight against the Summoner.

Combat phase 2 - at 50% health he will freeze everybody again and disengage, becoming untargetable as energy flows into him from his cultists in rooms 3 and 4.  Party needs to kill these adds before they can continue the fight.

Combat phase 3 - Summoner ‘absorbs’ energy from the cultist - gaining stats and doing more damage (maybe swap the mob out for a tougher one with a more demonic description?)

Combat phase 4 - As a Death trigger the Summoner gloats that he has already won, you are too late yadda yadda.  Party frozen while a scene plays out, Raven is resurrected.

Dungeon Phase 3.)

Raven fight - proper boss.

9 rooms, in a square, entrance appears down from the ritual room after the fight, Raven starts in the middle. Fleeing back up will be blocked.  Raven will chase, and will load darkling spawn adds to assist her. At 50% she will stop to cast a spell (trigger) that will damage everybody fighting her for 75 hp, and heal her for 200 hp.  Players can avoid this by fleeing off when the warning emote starts.
