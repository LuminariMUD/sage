# The Ages of Lumia

## The Structure of Time

The history of Lumia is divided into distinct Ages, each defined by the dominant cosmic force and ended by world-shaping cataclysms. These Ages represent not just the passage of time, but fundamental shifts in the nature of reality itself.

---

## The Primordial Era
*The Time Before Time*

### Nature of the Era
- No physical laws as currently understood
- Reality fluid and mutable
- Aeons and Khadhu roam freely across all planes
- No distinction between material and spiritual

### Key Characteristics
- **Duration**: Immeasurable
- **Dominant Force**: Raw creation and destruction in balance
- **Magic Level**: Infinite and unformed
- **Civilization**: None - no sentient life exists

### The Era Ends With
The Collision of Luminous and Coshek, birthing sentient life and beginning measured time.

---

## The Age of Unity
*The Brief Golden Moment*

### Nature of the Age
- Newly born Luminari explore their existence
- Free will emerges for the first time
- Powers of creation and destruction wielded without restraint
- No concept of individual vs. collective good

### Key Characteristics
- **Duration**: Mere moments to decades (unclear)
- **Dominant Force**: Unbridled potential
- **Magic Level**: Godlike and uncontrolled
- **Civilization**: Proto-divine collective

### The Prisoner's Warning
- Foresees the chaos that unrestrained power will bring
- Calls for voluntary binding and unity
- Rejected as attempted tyranny

### The Age Ends With
The First Schism - Luminari reject unity, declare the Prisoner enemy.

---

## The Age of the First War
*The War That Shaped Reality*

### Nature of the Age
- Reality itself weaponized in the conflict
- The Prisoner grows stronger with each absorption
- Other Luminari unite only when facing extinction
- Physical laws begin to solidify from the conflict

### Key Characteristics
- **Duration**: Unknown centuries or millennia
- **Dominant Force**: Conflict between Order (Prisoner) and Chaos (Luminari)
- **Magic Level**: Reality-breaking
- **Civilization**: None on Lumia yet

### The Discovery of Lumia
- World found with unique Arcanite properties
- Natural magical field can contain even godlike beings
- Becomes the chosen prison site

### The Age Ends With
The creation of the Arcanite Prison and imprisonment of the Prisoner.

---

## The Age of Vigil
*The Long Watch*

### Nature of the Age
- Luminari maintain the prison through constant effort
- Lumia's indigenous peoples develop independently
- Humans, elves, dwarves, orcs, halflings evolve
- Kingdoms rise and fall without divine interference

### Key Characteristics
- **Duration**: Several millennia
- **Dominant Force**: Divine maintenance of cosmic balance
- **Magic Level**: Locked away in the prison and Arcanite
- **Civilization**: Bronze to Iron Age development

### The Wandering
- Some Luminari grow weary of eternal vigilance
- Abandon posts to live among mortals
- Teach magic to indigenous peoples
- Begin of mortal magical traditions

### The Age Ends With
Too few Luminari remain to maintain the prison alone.

---

## The Age of Marking
*The Elevation of Mortals*

### Nature of the Age
- Mortals recruited to help maintain the prison
- The Mark of the Luminari creates first heroes
- Discovery of Arcanite's properties
- Rise of magical civilizations

### Key Characteristics
- **Duration**: 1000-2000 years
- **Dominant Force**: Mortal ambition guided by divine purpose
- **Magic Level**: High but controlled through Arcanite
- **Civilization**: Advanced magical societies

### The Arcana Golem Discovery
- Marked learn to transform bodies with Arcanite
- Creation of living weapons of war
- Power corrupts the Marked
- Competition for resources begins

### The Age Ends With
The Last War - Arcana Golems tear the world apart.

---

## The Age of Ash
*The Last War and Its Aftermath*

### Nature of the Age
- World-spanning conflict between Arcana Golem factions
- Skies darkened with ash and fire
- Mass extinction of species and civilizations
- Prison weakened by the devastation

### Key Characteristics
- **Duration**: 50-100 years of war, 400+ years of recovery
- **Dominant Force**: Uncontrolled destruction
- **Magic Level**: Catastrophic then greatly diminished
- **Civilization**: Collapse then slow rebuilding

### The Touch of Darkness
- Prisoner breaches prison slightly
- Creates first Darklings through corruption
- Luminari sacrifice themselves to reseal
- Only Serane remains

### Major Developments
- Crystal Dwarves seal themselves away
- Surface world loses most knowledge
- Magic becomes myth and legend
- New kingdoms slowly emerge

### The Age Ends With
Crystal Dwarves re-emerge after 500 years of isolation.

---

## The Age of Revelation
*Current Age - The Veil Lifts*

### Nature of the Age
- Hidden truths gradually revealed
- Ancient powers stirring
- Magic returning but Arcanite depleting
- Heroes marked for a new conflict

### Key Characteristics
- **Duration**: 80 years so far
- **Dominant Force**: Rediscovery and preparation
- **Magic Level**: Moderate but growing unstable
- **Civilization**: Renaissance-level with magical elements

### Current Status (Year 80 of the Revelation)
- Crystal Dwarves share pre-war knowledge
- Darklings increasingly active
- Serane marking new heroes
- Titans taking interest in mortal affairs
- The Shadowmoon approaching

### Signs of Change
- Arcanite depletion weakening prison
- Aggressive creatures at borders
- Prophecies activating
- Knight Orders reforming
- Ancient artifacts reawakening

### The Age Will End With
Unknown - prophecies suggest either:
- The Second Dawn (reunification of divine nature)
- The Final Liberation (Prisoner's escape)
- The Great Convergence (union of all orders)

---

## The Prophesied Age
*What May Come*

### Possible Futures

#### The Age of Oblivion
- If the Prisoner escapes
- Magic dies completely
- Return to primordial chaos
- Reality unmakes itself

#### The Age of Ascension
- If heroes prevent the escape
- Mortals transcend limitations
- New Luminari arise
- Prison transformed or eliminated

#### The Age of Balance
- If unity achieved between forces
- Creation and Oblivion reconciled
- New cosmic order established
- Mortals and divines as equals

---

## Cultural Perspectives on the Ages

### Crystal Dwarf Reckoning
- Count from "The Sealing" (when they closed their doors)
- Year 580 After Sealing (AS)
- Detailed records of pre-war era

### Human Kingdoms
- Count from "The Phoenix Rising" (Ashenport's founding)
- Year 234 of the New Calendar (NC)
- Myths and legends of earlier times

### Elven Memory
- Remember through ancestral dreams
- No numerical system - events marked by "Turnings"
- Still recall the Age of Vigil dimly

### The Marked
- Feel the ages through their soul-bond
- Experience visions of past ages
- Know time is running out

## Metaphysical Notes

### The Loom and Ages
Each Age represents a different "weaving pattern" in the Loom of Aether:
- **Primordial**: No pattern - raw threads
- **Unity**: First attempt at pattern
- **First War**: Pattern tears and reforms violently  
- **Vigil**: Careful, maintained pattern
- **Marking**: Mortal threads added to divine
- **Ash**: Pattern burns but doesn't break
- **Revelation**: Pattern fraying, new design emerging

### The Shadowmoon's Influence
- Appears every 3,000-5,000 years
- Each appearance coincides with Age transitions
- Weakens barriers between planes
- Makes the Loom "loose" and changeable
