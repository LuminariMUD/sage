# The Complete Timeline of Lumia

## Overview
The history of Lumia spans from the primordial creation through multiple ages of conflict, each marked by world-shaping cataclysms. This timeline chronicles the rise and fall of civilizations, the imprisonment of a cosmic threat, and the ongoing struggle between Creation and Oblivion.

---

## The Primordial Era
*Before Time Was Measured*

### The First Convergence
- **The Infinite Eternal** unleashes two primordial forces:
  - **Aeons**: Emissaries of creation (non-sentient)
  - **Khadhu**: Harbingers of destruction (non-sentient)
- These cosmic agents transcend all boundaries: physical, extraplanar, and spiritual

### The Cataclysmic Collision
- **Aeon Luminous** (creation) collides with **Khadhu Coshek** (destruction)
- The explosion births the **Luminari**: beings of fused physical form and eternal spirit
- From the remnants emerges **The Prisoner**: a Luminari composed of residual energies

### The First Schism
- The Prisoner foresees danger in uncontrolled free will
- Calls for unity and binding of powers to prevent cosmic catastrophe
- Other Luminari reject this as subjugation of their individuality
- **The First War of the Luminari** begins

---

## The Age of Imprisonment
*Unmeasured Eons Ago*

### The Great Conflict
- The Prisoner, forced into conflict, begins absorbing other Luminari
- Growing more powerful with each absorption
- Threatens to consume all existence to restore balance through Oblivion

### The Creation of the Prison
- Luminari unite against the existential threat
- Discover **Lumia**: a world with unique magical properties
- **Crystalline Arcanite** abundant on the planet creates strong magical fields
- Luminari sacrifice portions of their essence to create the **Arcanite Prison**
- **Five Locks** spread across the surface, keys hidden
- Prison's creation brings magic into the world

### The Vigil Begins
- Most Luminari dedicate themselves to maintaining the prison
- Some grow weary and integrate with indigenous peoples
- These wayward Luminari teach magic to mortals
- Ushers in new age of prosperity

---

## The Age of Marking
*Several Millennia Ago*

### The Decline of Guardians
- Wayward Luminari abandon their posts
- Remaining faithful too few to maintain the prison alone
- Decision made to recruit mortal assistance

### The Mark of the Luminari
- Faithful Luminari begin marking promising mortals
- **The Mark**: Binds soul to this life, grants rapid advancement in power
- Marked given duty to protect the prison and prevent the Prisoner's release
- Discovery of Arcanite's power to enhance items and bodies

### Rise of the Arcana Golems
- Marked discover ability to transform bodies using Arcanite
- **Arcana Golems**: More construct than mortal, city-leveling power
- Greed and lust for power emerge among the Marked
- Competition for Arcanite resources begins

---

## The Last War
*Approximately 500-1000 Years Ago*

### The Great Devastation
- War erupts between Marked factions over Arcanite
- Arcana Golems lead armies in world-spanning conflict
- Skies filled with fire and ash
- Civilizations crumble, lands scarred

### The Touch of the Prisoner
- Prison weakens during the chaos
- Prisoner forces prison open slightly
- **The Touch**: Corrupts souls of some Marked
- These become the first **Darklings** - twisted souls serving the Prisoner
- Darklings flee into hiding when discovered

### The Final Sacrifice
- All Luminari except one sacrifice themselves to restore the prison
- Their essence used to strengthen the weakened barriers
- **Serane**: The last Luminari, takes up solitary vigil
- Arcana Golems destroy each other in final battles
- Knowledge of creating Arcana Golems thought lost

---

## The Age of Isolation
*500 Years Ago to 80 Years Ago*

### The Crystal Dwarf Isolation: The Transformation of Nagburim
- When the Last War erupted, a clan of dwarves dwelling in the Crystal Caves sealed their gates
- They believed the surface world lost to ruin and flame
- Deep in caverns lit by veins of raw Arcanite, the transformation began
- The crystal's slow pulse reshaped them: flesh became translucent, hunger fell away
- What emerged were no longer dwarves but **Crystal Dwarves**—the Glassborn of legend
- Bodies of living crystal, hearts of Arcanite lattice sustaining their being
- Development of rigid caste system based on crystal purity
- The practice of gilding arose—tracing flaws with molten metal
- Language evolved into Tal, spoken through harmonic resonance
- Meticulous record-keeping preserves pre-war knowledge, including Luminari secrets

### Surface World Recovery
- Scattered survivors rebuild civilizations
- Formation of new kingdoms:
  - **Old Anteria** (later falls)
  - **Magocracy of Chulan**
  - **Cerax**
- Magic becomes legend and myth
- True history of the Last War fades from memory

### The First Darkling Wars
*Timeframe uncertain - possibly 200-300 years ago*

- Darklings emerge from hiding
- Begin sowing destruction and conflict
- **Creation of Half-Orcs**: Chulanese sorcerers combine orc strength with human intelligence
- Half-Orcs become shock troops for the Anterian Alliance
- Alliance eventually pushes back Darkling hordes

---

## The Age of Re-emergence
*80 Years Ago to Present*

### 80 Years Ago: The Crystal Dwarves Return
- The gates of Nagburim open after five centuries of silence
- Small group of surface dwarves rediscover their transformed kin
- Crystal Dwarves revealed to surface world—the Glassborn of legend made real
- Myths proven true: glowing hearts, harmonic voices, bodies of living crystal
- Crystal Dwarf records reveal lost history from before the Last War
- Knowledge of Arcana Golems partially recovered (though only lesser versions can be made)
- Miners' warnings gain new weight as Crystal Dwarves assert Arcanite monopoly
- The word "Glassborn" rejected by Crystal Dwarves as a name of ignorance

### The New Kingdoms
*Post-First Darkling Wars*

- **New Anteria** rises from Old Anteria's ashes
- **Ashenport** becomes the Great Port, center of civilization
- **Kohn**, **Sanctus** established
- **Mosswood** founded by Ashenport refugees

### Recent Developments
*Last 50 Years*

- Crystal Dwarves establish Arcanite monopoly
- Control mining to prevent racial extinction
- Surface races depend on Crystal Dwarf Arcanite supply

### Current Era
*Present Day*

- **Serane** begins marking new heroes
- Rising Darkling activity reported
- Arcanite depletion weakening the prison
- **The Shadowmoon** approaching (cycles every several thousand years)
- Aggressive creatures appearing at border regions
- Titans showing renewed interest in mortal affairs
