> After the Last War, everyone agreed civilization must be rebuilt.
>
> The trouble began when they asked what civilization was for.

## Three Answers to the Ash

Along the road between Ashenport and Mosswood, three young institutions have
grown around three old needs: shelter, law, and memory.

The **Mosswood Freehands** say civilization exists to protect people whom power
has found inconvenient.

The **Ashenport Civic Guard** says civilization exists to make protection a
duty rather than a favor.

The **League of the Lantern Atlas** says civilization exists to remember why
both promises fail.

They are not the largest powers in Lumia. None commands a nation, a Knight
Order, or a divine mandate. That is precisely why they matter. Cosmic crises
arrive first as missing travelers, corrupt patrols, empty grain stores, and a
ruin opened by someone who needed the money.

The Three Embers live where catastrophe is still small enough to know people's
names.

## The Mosswood Freehands

### Valariella Woodthimble

Valariella Woodthimble is a half-elven ranger with the courtesy of a drawn bow:
beautifully made, held under tension, and aimed before most people notice.

Her husband died during a necromancer's attack on an outlying Mosswood
settlement. The local wardens arrived after the danger had passed and before the
property claims began. Valariella never forgave the order of those events.

She trained farmers, charcoal burners, fungus tenders, refugees, and anyone else
who had been advised to wait for lawful protection. The first lessons were
archery and alarm calls. The second lessons were how to refuse a landlord. By
the time officials named her followers bandits, most of the northeast road was
safer than it had been under official patrol.

### The Tower Without a Master

The Freehands occupy the emptied tower of the necromancer whose violence first
made Valariella a widow. They call it **Hearthbreak**, because a refuge should
remember what required it.

The lower halls shelter travelers and displaced families. The upper rooms hold
training floors, seed stores, seized account books, and one chamber no Freehand
enters alone. A preserved heart beats behind the wall there. Valariella says it
belongs to the former master and is merely a remnant awaiting safe destruction.

The forest gives a different answer whenever lightning strikes the tower.

### Their Three Purposes

- **Public:** Teach ordinary settlements to survive threats without waiting for
  distant permission.
- **Private:** Build a chain of refuges independent of every nation and wealthy
  house.
- **Secret:** Determine whether Hearthbreak's lingering magic can protect those
  inside without teaching the refuge to become another prison.

The Freehands accept food, timber offered under Mosswood covenant, and defensive
tools. They are interested in Arcanite only as a means to brace shelters and
warning beacons. Valariella's enemies believe this distinction will last exactly
until she has enough crystal to become a government.

So do some of her friends.

## The Ashenport Civic Guard

### Captain Halden Alfarth

Halden Alfarth inherited a guard sword, a family posture, and the sincere belief
that a law applied unevenly is a law not yet properly enforced.

Ashenport has been educating him.

He rose through quay patrols by settling disputes without selling the weaker
party the privilege of being heard. Dockworkers trust him. Smugglers respect his
predictability. Several old houses praise him in public and keep careful lists
of where he drinks.

Halden's crisis is not that the Republic of Nine is a tyranny. It is that a
government designed to divide power can divide responsibility just as easily.
When guards abuse a ward, the mayor blames training, the noble seats blame
funding, the common seats blame appointments, and everyone arrives innocent at
the same injured door.

### The Broken Sword Hearing

During a public hearing, Halden presented evidence against officers protected by
one of the old houses. A chancellor asked whether the captain served written law
or personal conscience.

Halden laid his inherited sword across the witness rail and broke it with both
hands.

“I had hoped,” he said, “that they were the same blade.”

The mark remains in the rail. Half the city treats it as vandalism. The other
half touches it before giving testimony.

### Their Three Purposes

- **Public:** Protect residents, enforce Ashenport's charter, and keep road and
  harbor violence from becoming ordinary.
- **Private:** Rebuild the Guard's legitimacy through transparent hearings and
  ward oversight.
- **Secret:** Restore an abandoned Alfarth watch-fort beyond the east gate as a
  refuge and training ground should political protection inside the city fail.

Halden seeks Arcanite for walls, lamps, and warding, never personal enhancement.
He says this often enough that Professor Filindel has begun recording the exact
date on which necessity changes the sentence.

## The League of the Lantern Atlas

### Professor Filindel

Filindel is an elven archivist old enough to remember three scholarly fashions
declared eternal and young enough, by his people's measure, to be embarrassed by
all of them.

The League began as a room above an Ashenport chandlery. It now maintains maps,
oral histories, ruin rubbings, weather logs, and a catalogue of objects that
should not be removed until someone can explain why they were hidden.

Filindel's familiar and principal colleague is **Mira-of-the-Margin**, a small
winged intelligence who annotates his work while he is writing it. Most League
members consider her the better scholar. Filindel agrees in principle and edits
the meeting minutes afterward.

### The Archive That Defends Itself

Following repeated thefts, the League activated protections described in a
pre-war survey. The wards preserved every record.

They also began rearranging the collection according to a classification no
living member understands. Doors now open onto the subject a visitor fears
rather than the shelf requested. One chamber has attached itself to Mira's
shadow, making it impossible for her to leave the building without taking an
unknown portion of the archive with her.

Filindel wants to free her. He also wants to know what the chamber contains.

His shame lies in being uncertain which desire comes first.

### Their Three Purposes

- **Public:** Map unsafe roads, recover endangered history, and make practical
  knowledge available to settlements beyond wealthy libraries.
- **Private:** Reconstruct the Luminari and Darkling history lost after the Last
  War.
- **Secret:** Determine whether Arcana Golem designs hidden in the collection
  can give the archive a guardian without giving an old war-purpose a new body.

The League seeks Arcanite first as evidence and only second as instrument. This
order of priority comforts no one who has watched Filindel encounter a sealed
door.

## The Ashfall Compact

The three factions cooperate under an agreement written after a creature raid
left an eastern road settlement burning.

The Freehands evacuated households through the forest. The Guard held the road.
The League identified the attackers as Darkling-touched animals following a
pre-war boundary marker rather than a living commander. Each faction had the
part of the answer the others distrusted.

Their compact requires:

1. warnings and safe-route maps to be shared without price;
2. civilians to be moved before relics, evidence, or arrests;
3. any use of Arcanite to be recorded and reported to the Crystal Reaches;
4. each faction to keep one observer in the councils of the other two;
5. no faction to claim sole credit while the dead remain unnamed.

They violate the fourth clause frequently and the fifth almost beautifully.

## The Ember Cache

_The following section is restricted._

Beneath a ruined tollhouse between Ashenport and Mosswood lies a small pre-war
Arcanite cache. It is not a natural vein. Each crystal has been cut into a
curving segment, as though all once belonged to a single spherical lattice.

Valariella believes the pieces could ward a refuge. Halden believes they must be
held under civic seal. Filindel believes the object should be reconstructed long
enough to identify and then dismantled in a more educated manner.

All three know extraction from the cache may weaken something larger.

None knows what the original lattice did.

Darkling agents have encouraged each faction to believe one of the others has
already stolen a piece. The rumor is false.

For now.

The cache is sometimes called a **key** in Althair Lenadrian's notes. This is an
unverified interpretation, not evidence that any material device is one of the
Five Locks or can open the Prison. The canonical Locks remain Memory, Identity,
Time, Space, and Will.

## Standing Among the Embers

The Three Embers grant trust before authority. A proven ally may carry a title,
hold resources, speak in council, or lead a bounded undertaking. None permits an
outsider to replace the institution's accountable officers by accumulating
favors alone.

This is not merely caution. Each faction was founded because charismatic power
failed someone. They will gladly honor heroes.

They are less eager to be owned by one.

## Provenance and Promotion Decisions

This document rewrites the setting material in the retired
`lore_docs/drafts/factions/Faction Overview.md` within the established
Ashenport, Mosswood, Five Nations, Arcanite, and Darkling canon.

Valariella Woodthimble, Halden Alfarth, Professor Filindel, their founding
wounds, their widening ambitions, Hearthbreak, the broken family sword, the
threatened archive, and their competing interest in Arcanite are promoted.
Alignment labels, staff-assigned NPC control, event scheduling, mechanical
benefits, algorithmic reputation, predetermined moral outcomes, and the proposal
to make Darklings a conventional joinable faction are retired.
